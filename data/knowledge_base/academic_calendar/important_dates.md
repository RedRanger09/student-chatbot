# Important Academic Dates

## Annual Timeline

**Admission**:  
Application deadlines fall between **March 1st - April 30th** for undergraduate programs and **February 1st - March 31st** for postgraduate admissions. Late applications may incur a **25% fee penalty** and are only accepted until **May 15th**, after which no further applications are processed.

**Registration**:  
New students must complete online registration between **April 10th - May 5th**. Late registration (June 1st-10th) requires a **$50 fee** and is limited to **20% capacity**. Course selection deadlines are **May 5th**, with no changes allowed after **May 8th**.

**Fee Payment**:  
Tuition fees must be paid in full by **June 30th**. Partial payments accepted until **July 15th** but incur a **5% monthly interest rate**. Late payment penalties apply from **August 1st**, with a **$200 fee** for delayed payments beyond **September 1st**.

**Add/Drop**:  
Students can add courses until **June 30th** and drop courses without penalty until **July 15th**. Drops after **July 15th** require department approval and result in a **"W" grade**, which may impact GPA calculations.

## Semester Deadlines

**Assignment Deadlines**:  
Mid-semester assignments are due every **Monday at 11:59 PM** throughout the term. Late submissions incur a **10% penalty per day** up to **5 days**, after which they are marked as **"NF" (Not Found)**.

**Practical Exams**:  
Laboratory and practical exams occur biweekly starting from **August 20th**. Missed exams without prior approval are recorded as **"Fail"**, requiring retakes during the **exam revision period**.

**Study Leave**:  
Requests for study leave must be submitted by **October 15th** with supporting documentation. Approval is granted on a case-by-case basis, and approved leaves require **weekly progress reports** to remain valid.

## Examination Deadlines

**Mid-Semester Exams**:  
These occur in **November** covering 40% of the syllabus. Results are published by **December 5th**, with revaluation applications due by **December 12th**. Supplementary exams for failed papers must be taken by **January 31st**.

**End-Semester Exams**:  
Final examinations run from **February 10th - 28th**. Result publication happens by **March 15th**, with revaluation applications due by **March 22nd**. Supplementary exams for final results are scheduled between **April 5th - 15th**.

## Graduation Deadlines

**Graduation Application**:  
Students must apply for graduation by **March 31st** with a completed **Degree Audit Report (DAR)**. Late applications (April 1st-15th) require departmental approval and incur a **$100 fee**.

**Degree Issuance**:  
Certificates are issued within **45 days** of application submission. Graduates must attend the **convocation ceremony** on **May 20th**, with no exceptions for missed dates unless documented emergencies occur.

## Important Reminders

- All deadlines are subject to change based on university announcements
- Email notifications will be sent 7 days before each major deadline
- Students must check their myUni portal for real-time updates
- Late submissions without prior approval will not be accepted
- Attendance records are monitored throughout the semester

⚠️ **Fee payment** must be completed before course registration  
⚠️ **Mid-semester exams** require prior approval for absences  
⚠️ **Final results** determine eligibility for honors programs  
⚠️ **Graduation applications** must include proof of cleared academic standing

## What Happens If a Deadline Is Missed

- **Admission**: No application processing, automatic disqualification
- **Registration**: Limited course options, potential loss of scholarships
- **Fee Payment**: Late fees apply, possible suspension from classes
- **Add/Drop**: "W" grade recorded, impact on GPA and financial aid
- **Assignment Submissions**: Marked as "NF", no rework allowed
- **Exams**: Fail grade awarded, retake required during supplementary period
- **Graduation**: Delayed certificate issuance, possible loss of eligibility for honors

## Important Student Recommendations

1. **Set calendar reminders** 7 days before all major deadlines  
2. **Submit assignments early** to avoid technical issues  
3. **Monitor email notifications** for last-minute changes  
4. **Maintain a study schedule** to meet all academic requirements  
5. **Consult with academic advisors** for personalized guidance  
6. **Keep track of attendance records** throughout the semester  
7. **Review syllabi thoroughly** to understand all course requirements
