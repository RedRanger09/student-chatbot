### Course Registration
Students must register for courses during the designated registration periods. Late registrations may incur fees or result in loss of enrollment rights. Course selection is finalized during the Add/Drop period, which allows students to adjust their schedules before classes begin.

### Orientation
Orientation week provides an introduction to university policies, academic expectations, and available resources. Both virtual and on-campus participation options are offered to accommodate diverse student needs.

### Regular Classes
Classes commence on the first day of the semester and follow a structured weekly schedule. Attendance is mandatory unless excused by the university or faculty.

### Internal Assessments
Internal assessments, including quizzes and assignments, are conducted throughout the semester to monitor student progress and provide feedback for improvement.

### Mid-Semester Examinations
Mid-semester examinations occur approximately halfway through the academic term. These assessments evaluate students' understanding of core concepts and serve as a checkpoint for academic performance.

### Practical Examinations
Practical examinations or project submissions are due within specified deadlines. These evaluations focus on applied skills and real-world problem-solving capabilities.

### Assignment Deadlines
All assignments must be submitted by their respective deadlines. Late submissions may result in reduced grades or penalties, depending on institutional policies.

### Study Leave
Students may request optional study leave during designated periods to pursue internships, personal development, or other academic-related activities. Approval from academic advisors is required.

### End-Semester Examinations
Final examinations are conducted near the end of each semester and cover the entire curriculum. These assessments determine final grades and are critical for academic progression.

### Result Declaration
Results are officially declared on specified dates, with final grades published on university portals. Students may appeal results within the supplementary examination window if eligible.

### Supplementary Examinations
Supplementary examinations are offered to students who fail main exams but meet specific criteria. These opportunities ensure equitable assessment and academic continuity.

### Semester Break
Semester breaks provide students with time to rest, engage in personal development, or pursue internships. These periods also allow faculty to prepare for the next semester's curriculum.

### Holidays
Holidays are observed during the academic calendar, with adjustments made to the schedule as needed. Students should consult the university calendar for specific dates.

### Graduation Activities (Optional)
Graduation ceremonies and related activities are optional but encouraged for students completing their degrees. These events mark significant milestones in academic careers.

## Important Notes

1. **Registration Deadlines:** All course registrations must be completed by the specified dates to avoid late fees or loss of enrollment rights.
2. **Mid-Semester Examinations:** These are designed to assess understanding of core concepts and provide feedback for improvement before final exams.
3. **Study Leave:** Students may request optional study leave during designated periods, subject to approval by academic advisors.
4. **Final Examinations:** These are comprehensive assessments covering the entire semester's curriculum. Students must prepare thoroughly using revision resources provided by faculty.
5. **Result Declaration:** Results are published on official university portals and are final unless appealed within the supplementary examination window.
6. **Semester Breaks:** These periods allow students to rest, pursue internships, or engage in personal development activities.

## Student Responsibilities

- **Adhere to Academic Calendar:** Students must follow the schedule strictly to avoid penalties for late submissions or missed deadlines.
- **Attend Orientation:** Participation in orientation is mandatory to familiarize oneself with university policies, resources, and academic expectations.
- **Manage Time Effectively:** Balancing coursework, assignments, and personal commitments is crucial for academic success.
- **Seek Clarification:** Students should consult faculty or academic advisors for any uncertainties regarding deadlines, grading, or course requirements.
- **Participate in Examinations:** All students are required to appear for scheduled examinations unless exempted by the university.

---

## Common Scheduling Mistakes

1. **Ignoring Add/Drop Deadlines:** Missing the add/drop window may result in automatic withdrawal from courses.
2. **Underestimating Exam Preparation Time:** Final exams require extensive revision, and last-minute cramming often leads to poor performance.
3. **Not Planning for Study Leave:** Students who fail to plan for optional study leave risk falling behind in coursework.
4. **Neglecting Practical Deadlines:** Project or practical work deadlines are often overlooked, leading to incomplete submissions.
5. **Overlooking Supplementary Examinations:** Results declared after the main examination window are only valid if students qualify for supplementary exams.
