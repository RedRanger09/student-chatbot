## Withdrawal Policy
Withdrawing from a course after the drop period but before the final deadline will result in a "W" grade on the transcript. This does not impact GPA but may affect financial aid eligibility. Students should consult with their academic advisor before withdrawing.

## Backlog Policy
Students who accumulate more than 15% of their required credit hours in backlog (courses repeated due to failure) are placed on academic probation. They must:
- Complete a minimum of 30 credits per semester.
- Achieve a cumulative GPA of 2.0 or higher.
- Submit an academic improvement plan.

## Academic Standing
Academic standing is determined by:
- Cumulative GPA
- Credit hour completion
- Compliance with university policies

Students may be placed on:
- **Good Standing**: GPA ≥ 2.0 and no unresolved violations.
- **Academic Probation**: GPA < 2.0 or backlog exceeding 15%.
- **Academic Suspension**: GPA < 1.5 or failure to meet probation requirements.

## Academic Integrity
All students are expected to uphold the highest standards of academic honesty. Violations include:
- Plagiarism
- Cheating on exams
- Fabrication of data
- Unauthorized collaboration

Penalties for violations range from warnings to expulsion, depending on severity and repeat offenses.

## Graduation Requirements
To graduate, students must:
1. Complete all required credit hours (typically 120 credits).
2. Maintain a minimum GPA of 2.0.
3. Pass all required courses with a grade of "C" or higher.
4. Submit a graduation application by the specified deadline.

## Student Responsibilities
Students are expected to:
- Attend all classes and participate in academic activities.
- Complete assignments and exams on time.
- Adhere to university policies and codes of conduct.
- Seek assistance from academic advisors when needed.

## Academic Appeals
Students may appeal decisions related to academic standing, course withdrawals, or grading disputes. The process involves:
1. Submitting a written appeal to the relevant department within 10 business days of the decision.
2. Attending a review meeting with faculty and/or the appeals committee.
3. Receiving a final determination within 30 days.

## Common Mistakes
Common errors students make include:
- Missing registration deadlines
- Failing to complete required courses
- Not seeking academic advising
- Violating plagiarism policies
- Misunderstanding withdrawal consequences
