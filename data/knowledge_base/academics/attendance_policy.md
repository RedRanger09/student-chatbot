# University Attendance Policy

## Overview
This policy establishes guidelines for student attendance at all academic activities including lectures, seminars, laboratories, and fieldwork. The primary objectives are to:
- Promote a culture of academic responsibility
- Ensure equitable treatment of students
- Maintain educational quality through consistent participation
- Provide mechanisms for exceptional circumstances

The policy applies to all undergraduate and graduate programs offered by the university. Attendance is a critical component of academic success, as regular participation facilitates knowledge acquisition, skill development, and peer interaction.

## Minimum Attendance Requirements
Students must achieve a minimum attendance rate of **75%** across all scheduled classes for each course to be eligible for assessment. This threshold ensures:
- Sufficient exposure to instructional content
- Active participation in collaborative learning
- Timely completion of formative assessments

Courses with lab components require **100% attendance** at all practical sessions. For courses with fieldwork or clinical placements, attendance must meet the specific requirements outlined in the course syllabus.

## Attendance Recording System
The university employs a centralized digital system for tracking attendance:
1. **Biometric Scanning**: Mandatory for all classes
2. **Electronic Sign-In**: Available for students unable to use biometrics
3. **Faculty Reporting**: Professors submit daily attendance records

Students can access their attendance records through the Student Portal under "Academic Records". Monthly reports are also sent to faculty advisors.

## Medical Leave Policy
Students requiring medical leave must:
1. Submit a doctor's certificate within 3 business days of absence
2. Provide evidence of illness preventing class attendance
3. Obtain approval from the Dean's Office

Medical absences are excused but require documentation. Students on medical leave may need to complete alternative assignments or attend make-up sessions as arranged with faculty.

## Official Leave Policy
For official leave (e.g., family emergencies, religious observances):
1. Submit a written request to the Registrar's Office
2. Provide supporting documentation
3. Obtain approval from the Dean's Office

Official leave is granted for up to 2 weeks per academic year. Students on official leave must maintain contact with faculty and complete all assigned work within the approved timeframe.

## Attendance Shortage Consequences
Recurring unexcused absences may result in:
- **First offense**: Verbal warning from faculty
- **Second offense**: Written warning and mandatory counseling
- **Third offense**: Course failure and academic probation

Students accumulating 15 or more unexcused absences across all courses will be ineligible for examinations.

## Condonation Process
Condonation (waiver of attendance requirements) is available under exceptional circumstances:
1. Submit a formal request to the Academic Committee
2. Provide supporting documentation
3. Attend a review meeting with faculty representatives

Approval requires demonstration of extenuating circumstances that prevented regular attendance while maintaining academic progress.

## Examination Eligibility
Students must meet these criteria to be eligible for examinations:
- Achieved minimum attendance requirements
- Submitted all required assignments on time
- Completed any mandated make-up work
- Maintained satisfactory academic standing

Examinations will not be offered for courses where attendance requirements were not met, unless condonation is granted.

## Common Problems and Solutions
1. **Confusing Attendance Policies**: Review the official policy document
2. **Technical Issues with Attendance System**: Contact IT Support
3. **Medical Leave Documentation**: Visit the Health Center for assistance
4. **Make-Up Work Requests**: Submit through the Student Portal
5. **Attendance Discrepancies**: Contact the Registrar's Office

## FAQ

### 1. What is considered attendance?
All scheduled classes, including lectures, labs, and fieldwork, count toward your attendance record.

### 2. Can I miss a class without penalty?
Only excused absences (medical, official, or pre-approved) are permitted without academic consequences.

### 3. How do I request medical leave?
Submit a doctor's note to the Dean's Office within 3 business days of absence.

### 4. What if I can't attend all lab sessions?
Lab attendance is mandatory. Absences must be documented and approved through the official process.

### 5. Can I get credit for missed classes?
Make-up work may be available, but this is determined on a case-by-case basis by faculty.

### 6. How do I request official leave?
Submit a written request to the Registrar's Office with supporting documentation.

### 7. What happens if I miss too many classes?
Recurring unexcused absences can lead to academic probation or course failure.

### 8. Can I get an extension for missed assignments?
Extensions are granted only for documented medical or official leave situations.

### 9. How do I check my attendance record?
Access your records through the Student Portal under "Academic Records".

### 10. What is condonation?
Condonation is a waiver of attendance requirements for exceptional circumstances.

### 11. How long does condonation last?
Approved condonations are valid for the duration of the course.

### 12. Can I attend classes remotely?
Remote participation options may be available for specific courses; check with your faculty representative.

### 13. What if I have a religious observance conflict?
Submit a request to the Registrar's Office at least two weeks in advance.

### 14. How do I get help with attendance issues?
Contact your academic advisor or the Student Support Services office.

### 15. Can I attend classes on weekends?
Weekend classes are mandatory unless specifically excused.

### 16. What if I have a family emergency?
Submit an official leave request through the Registrar's Office.

### 17. How do I get a replacement for lost attendance records?
Request a duplicate from the Registrar's Office with valid identification.

### 18. Can I attend classes in another country?
International students must follow specific procedures; consult with the International Office.

### 19. What if I'm ill on exam day?
Submit a doctor's note immediately and contact your faculty representative.

### 20. How do I request accommodations for disabilities?
Contact the Disability Services Office to discuss reasonable accommodations.

### 21. Can I take exams without attending classes?
No, examination eligibility requires meeting attendance requirements unless condonation is granted.

### 22. What if I have a conflict with a scheduled class?
Check for alternative scheduling options or submit a request for course adjustments.

### 23. How do I report technical issues with the attendance system?
Contact the IT Help Desk for assistance with system access problems.

### 24. Can I get credit for prior coursework?
Credit transfer policies are reviewed on an individual basis by the Academic Committee.

### 25. What if I'm unsure about attendance requirements?
Review the official policy document or consult with your academic advisor.
