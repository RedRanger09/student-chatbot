# Admission Policies

## Eligibility Policy
- Minimum academic qualifications (e.g., high school diploma or equivalent)
- Language proficiency requirements (if applicable)
- Specific program prerequisites (e.g., work experience, portfolio submission)
- Citizenship/residency status criteria

## Application Fee Policy
- Standard application fee: $100 USD
- Waivers available for:
  - Students from low-income backgrounds
  - Alumni of affiliated institutions
  - Participants in specific outreach programs
- Payment methods: Online credit card, bank transfer, or check

## Refund Policy
- Full refund available if request received **7 days before** deadline
- 50% refund for requests received **3-6 days before** deadline
- No refunds after deadline unless extenuating circumstances documented

## Document Verification Policy
- Required documents:
  - Official transcripts
  - Degree certificates (for graduates)
  - Language proficiency test scores
  - Recommendation letters
  - Personal statement
- Documents must be:
  - Original or officially notarized copies
  - In English or with certified translation
  - Submitted through official channels

## Duplicate Applications
- Submissions received within **10 days** of each other will be flagged
- Institutional applications automatically checked for duplicates
- Applicants may request manual review via written appeal

## False Information Policy
- Fraudulent submissions will result in:
  - Immediate application rejection
  - Ban from future admissions processes
  - Notification to relevant academic institutions
- Suspicious documents trigger automated verification checks

## Admission Cancellation
- Cancelled for:
  - Non-payment of fees by deadline
  - Fraudulent activity detected
  - Incomplete documentation
  - Violation of program-specific requirements
- Notifications sent **5 days** before cancellation date

## Waiting List Policy
- Placement on waiting list is automatic for:
  - Applicants with minor deficiencies
  - Candidates whose applications are pending verification
- Regular updates provided via email
- Priority consideration given to candidates who:
  - Submit additional materials
  - Demonstrate exceptional circumstances

## Deferral Policy
- One-time deferral allowed for:
  - Health-related reasons (requires medical documentation)
  - Military service commitment
  - Work obligations in developing countries
- Deferred admission must be requested **before** final decision
- New application required for subsequent admission cycle

## Appeals Process
- Appeals must be submitted in writing within **14 days** of decision
- Include:
  - Detailed explanation of circumstances
  - Supporting documentation
  - Requested outcome
- Reviewed by admissions committee within **21 days**

## International Admission Policy
- Additional requirements for international applicants:
  - Valid visa sponsorship confirmation
  - Proof of financial support ($20k+ annual demonstration)
  - English language proficiency (minimum IELTS 6.5 or equivalent)
  - Cultural orientation program participation
- Special considerations for refugees/asylum seekers

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Can I apply for multiple programs?**  
A: Yes, but each application must be submitted separately with appropriate fee.

**Q: What if I miss the deadline?**  
A: Late applications are reviewed on a case-by-case basis with full fees applicable.

**Q: Are there any special considerations for mature students?**  
A: Yes - applicants over 25 years old may request portfolio review instead of standardized tests.

**Q: How do I request a fee waiver?**  
A: Submit a formal request with supporting documentation to admissions@univ.edu
