# University Admissions Handbook

## Overview
The University Admissions Process is designed to ensure that all applicants meet the academic and administrative requirements for enrollment. This handbook outlines the procedures, eligibility criteria, required documentation, and key policies governing the admissions process.

## Eligibility
To be considered for admission, applicants must:
- Complete the online application form
- Submit official academic transcripts
- Provide proof of English language proficiency (unless exempt)
- Pay the non-refundable application fee
- Meet minimum GPA requirements

International students must also:
- Demonstrate financial capacity to cover tuition and living expenses
- Submit additional documentation as specified by [Admissions Office]

## Required Documents
The following documents are required for a complete application:
1. Completed application form
2. Official academic transcripts from all post-secondary institutions attended
3. Standardized test scores (if applicable)
4. Letter of recommendation
5. Personal statement
6. Proof of financial capacity
7. English language proficiency test results

## Application Process
1. Visit the [ERP Portal] to create an account
2. Complete the online application form
3. Upload all required documents
4. Pay the non-refundable application fee
5. Submit the application for review
6. Wait for admission decision (typically 4-6 weeks)

## Important Policies
- All applicants must disclose any prior academic misconduct
- Application fees are non-refundable
- Deadlines are strictly enforced
- International students must maintain full-time enrollment status

## Deadlines
- Early Action Deadline: November 15
- Regular Decision Deadline: January 15
- Final Deadline for international applications: March 1

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the application fee?
The non-refundable application fee is $75 USD. Fee waivers are available for eligible applicants through [Scholarship Cell].

### Can I apply as an international student?
Yes, international students are welcome to apply. Additional documentation may be required as specified by [Admissions Office].

### What if I don't meet the GPA requirement?
Applicants with below-average GPAs may still qualify through:
1. Submission of additional academic work
2. Demonstration of relevant experience
3. Strong letters of recommendation

### How do I submit transcripts?
Official transcripts must be submitted through [ERP Portal] in PDF format. Hard copies should be mailed to [Registrar Office].

### What if I need an extension?
Extensions are granted only for documented emergencies. Contact [Student Affairs Office] immediately.

### Can I apply for multiple programs?
Yes, applicants may apply to up to three programs. Indicate your program preferences on the application form.

### What is the difference between Early Action and Regular Decision?
Early Action applications receive priority consideration and must be submitted by November 15. Regular Decision applications are reviewed after all complete applications are received.

### How do I check my application status?
Monitor your [ERP Portal] account for updates. Contact [Admissions Office] for urgent inquiries.

### What if I have extenuating circumstances?
Documented extenuating circumstances may affect admission decisions. Provide supporting evidence through [Scholarship Cell].

### Can I request a review of my application?
Yes, applicants may request a review within 30 days of the decision date. Submit your request to [Registrar Office].

### What are the consequences of submitting false information?
Submission of falsified documents may result in immediate disqualification and potential legal action.

### How do I apply for financial aid?
Complete the FAFSA form through [Fee Portal] and submit it with your application.

### Can I defer my admission?
Deferrals are granted for documented reasons such as military service or health issues. Submit a request to [Student Affairs Office].

### What if I need accommodations?
Contact [Scholarship Cell] to request academic accommodations. Provide documentation from an approved disability services provider.

### How do I cancel my application?
Applicants may withdraw their application within 14 days of submission by contacting [Admissions Office].

### Can I appeal a denied admission decision?
Appeals must be submitted in writing to [Registrar Office] within 30 days of the decision date.

### What is the minimum English proficiency requirement?
Non-native speakers must demonstrate proficiency through TOEFL (minimum 90) or IELTS (minimum 6.5).

### How do I submit my recommendation letters?
Letters should be submitted by the recommender through [ERP Portal]. Hard copies may be mailed to [Admissions Office].

### What if I have questions about my application?
Contact the [Admissions Office] during regular business hours for assistance.

### Can I request a meeting with an admissions representative?
Yes, schedule a virtual consultation through [ERP Portal]. Representatives are available for limited appointments.

### What if I need to submit documents in languages other than English?
All documents must be translated into English by an official translator. Submit both the original and translated versions.
