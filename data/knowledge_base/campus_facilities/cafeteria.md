# University Cafeteria Guide

## Overview
The University Cafeteria is a central hub for students to enjoy nutritious meals, snacks, and beverages in a welcoming environment. Located on campus, it offers a diverse range of dining options to accommodate various tastes and dietary needs. With modern facilities, friendly staff, and a commitment to food safety, the cafeteria aims to provide an exceptional dining experience for all students.

## Dining Services
### Food Courts
The cafeteria features a vibrant food court with multiple stalls offering a variety of cuisines, including Indian, Chinese, Italian, and fast-food options. Each stall specializes in different dishes, allowing students to explore global flavors while enjoying competitive pricing.

### Menu Categories
- **Main Courses**: Includes vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and premium options.
- **Snacks & Sides**: Available throughout the day, featuring chips, fruits, and drinks.
- **Desserts**: A selection of cakes, ice creams, and pastries to satisfy sweet cravings.
- **Specialty Items**: Daily themed dishes and seasonal favorites.

### Examples of Services
- **Meal Vouchers**: Pre-loaded cards for easy payment.
- **Self-Service Kiosks**: Streamline your order with touch-screen options.
- **Dietary Customization**: Inform staff about allergies or preferences for tailored meals.

## Meal Timings
The cafeteria operates during the following hours to accommodate academic schedules:
- **Breakfast**: 7:00 AM – 9:30 AM
- **Lunch**: 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM
- **Snacks**: 10:00 AM, 3:00 PM, and 6:00 PM
- **Dinner**: 5:30 PM – 8:00 PM

Special events or exams may adjust these timings; check the notice board for updates.

## Payment
### Methods
Students can pay using:
- **Cash** (limited to ₹1,000 notes)
- **Debit/Credit Cards**
- **Mobile Wallets** (Paytm, Google Pay, PhonePe)
- **Meal Vouchers**

### Student Discounts
- **Discounted Meals**: 15% off during midday meals (12:00 PM – 2:30 PM).
- **Weekend Passes**: Buy 3 meals, get the 4th free on Fridays and Saturdays.

## Hygiene
### Standards
- All kitchen staff undergo regular health checks.
- Utensils and surfaces are sanitized after every use.
- Food is stored in temperature-controlled environments to prevent contamination.

### Food Safety
- Fresh ingredients are used daily.
- Staff wear gloves and masks while handling food.
- Complaints about hygiene can be reported via the feedback form or directly to the cafeteria manager.

### Seating Arrangements
- **Indoor Tables**: Available for group meals (max 6 people).
- **Outdoor Benches**: Ideal for quick bites during breaks.
- **Private Dining**: Reserved for events and special occasions.

## Contact Information
### Operating Hours
- **Monday–Friday**: 7:00 AM – 8:30 PM
- **Saturday–Sunday**: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

### Complaint Process
To file a complaint:
1. Fill out the feedback form at the service desk.
2. Include details about the issue (e.g., food quality, hygiene, staff behavior).
3. Receive a response within 48 hours.

### Lost Items
- **Lost & Found**: Located near the entrance. Check here for misplaced belongings.
- **Reporting**: Inform staff if you’ve lost something; they’ll assist in locating it.

### Special Dietary Requests
Students with specific dietary needs (e.g., gluten-free, halal) should notify the cafeteria manager at least 24 hours in advance to arrange customized meals.

### Common Student Questions
1. **Can I bring my own food?**  
   Yes, but only for consumption in designated areas. No outside containers are allowed in dining zones.

2. **What if I have an allergy?**  
   Contact the manager to discuss your requirements. They’ll ensure your meal is prepared safely.

3. **How do I get a refund for a faulty item?**  
   Report the issue within 1 hour of purchase. The staff will replace it or offer a store credit.
