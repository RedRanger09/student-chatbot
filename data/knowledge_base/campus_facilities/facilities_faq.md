# Campus Facilities FAQ

## 1. How do I apply for a bus pass?  
To apply for a bus pass, visit the campus transportation office during business hours with your student ID. You’ll need to fill out an application form, pay the annual fee, and submit proof of enrollment. Passes are valid for the academic year and allow unlimited rides on all scheduled buses. Replacement passes cost $10 if lost or damaged.

## 2. Can I change my assigned bus route?  
Yes, but only during the first month of the semester. Submit a written request to the transportation office with valid reasons (e.g., new residence). They’ll review your case and adjust your route if feasible. Changes after this period require a formal appeal.

## 3. What are the bus service hours?  
Buses operate from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily, including weekends. Routes run every 15 minutes during peak hours (7:00–8:00 AM and 4:00–5:00 PM). Reduced frequency occurs during off-peak times. Check the campus app for real-time updates.

## 4. How do I join a sports club?  
Visit the student union’s website to browse available clubs. Most clubs require an application form and a minimum attendance of three meetings per month. Some may have tryouts or skill assessments. Joining fees vary by club but typically range from $20–$50.

## 5. Is the gym free for students?  
The main gym is free for all enrolled students with a valid ID. Additional facilities like the wellness center and pool require a $30 monthly membership. Discounts are available for athletes or those attending three+ fitness classes weekly.

## 6. What equipment can I borrow from the gym?  
Students may borrow resistance bands, yoga mats, and basic weights for personal use. Heavy equipment (e.g., barbells) requires instructor supervision. All borrowed items must be returned by the end of each semester to avoid late fees.

## 7. How do I book a sports facility?  
Use the campus booking portal to reserve courts, tracks, or training rooms. Reservations are first-come, first-served and require a deposit of $10 per session. Popular facilities fill up quickly, so book at least two weeks in advance.

## 8. Are there guest passes for visitors?  
Guests may use the campus bus system with a valid ID from their host. They must register at the transportation office with the host’s details and pay a one-time $5 fee. Passes are only valid for three days and require prior approval from the host.

## 9. What are the cafeteria opening hours?  
The main dining hall is open 7:00 AM–8:00 PM daily, including weekends. Extended hours (4:00–6:00 PM) are available during exam periods. Self-service kiosks operate 24/7 in the student lounge area.

## 10. Can I use pre-ordered meal plans at the cafeteria?  
Yes, but only for meals included in your plan. You may add cash or card payments for extra items. Meal plan balances are tracked through your student account and automatically deducted when you swipe your ID at any campus dining location.

## 11. How do I report a food allergy at the cafeteria?  
Inform staff at the point of service with your allergen details. The kitchen team will prepare a separate meal using substitute ingredients. For severe allergies, request an EpiPen from the health center and provide documentation to the cafeteria manager.

## 12. What payment methods are accepted in the cafeteria?  
Cash, campus debit card, and contactless payments (Apple Pay/Google Wallet) are accepted. Students with financial aid may use their ID for automatic deductions. Mobile ordering via the campus app is also available.

## 13. How do I connect to the campus Wi-Fi?  
Open your device’s Wi-Fi settings and select "CampusNet." Enter your student ID as the password. If you’re a guest, choose "GuestNet" and follow the registration prompts on the login page.

## 14. Why can't I access the campus network?  
Common issues include incorrect passwords, expired credentials, or device registration problems. Check your ID validity through the portal. If unresolved, contact IT support with your device details for troubleshooting.

## 15. Can guests use the university Wi-Fi?  
Guests may access "GuestNet" for up to two hours without registration. To extend usage, they must complete a guest form at the IT desk with their name and purpose of visit. Monthly passes cost $20 per person.

## 16. How do I reset my campus network password?  
Visit the IT service portal to request a password reset. Your student ID will be verified, and a temporary code sent to your email. If you’ve forgotten your ID, contact the registrar’s office for assistance.

## 17. What should I do if my device is blocked from Wi-Fi?  
Ensure your device is registered in the campus system. Unplug and reconnect to the network. If issues persist, submit a support ticket with your device make/model and error messages. IT will investigate and resolve within 24 hours.

## 18. How do I request a new computer lab?  
Submit a formal proposal through the facilities management portal. Include expected usage numbers, required software, and suggested location. Approval typically takes four weeks, after which construction begins.

## 19. What are the rules for using campus printers?  
Print jobs must be submitted via the campus app or web portal. Color printing costs $0.25 per page; black-and-white is $0.10. All prints are subject to a $10 monthly limit unless you purchase a print credit package.

## 20. Can I use my personal laptop in classrooms?  
Yes, but only if it meets the minimum system requirements (Windows 10 or macOS Catalina). Devices must be registered through the IT portal to access campus software licenses. Laptops without registration may face connectivity issues.

## 21. How do I request a classroom projector upgrade?  
Fill out the facilities request form with specific details about your needs. The campus will prioritize requests based on usage frequency and budget availability. Expect a response within three weeks of submission.

## 22. What is the policy for late library returns?  
A $0.50 fee applies per day overdue, up to a maximum of $15. Items not returned by the final due date are considered lost, and replacement fees apply. Renewals are available online if no one has placed a hold.

## 23. Can I access campus resources remotely?  
Yes, through the virtual campus portal. This includes library databases, academic software licenses, and collaboration tools. Remote access requires multi-factor authentication and is limited to approved devices.

## 24. How do I report a broken classroom device?  
Use the maintenance request form in the facilities app. Include photos of the damage and your contact information. The campus will schedule repairs within five business days. Emergency issues may receive priority treatment.

## 25. What are the hours for the student lounge?  
The lounge is open 24/7 with staff available during regular office hours (8:00 AM–6:00 PM). Late-night study sessions are encouraged, but noise complaints must be reported to security within one hour of occurrence.

## 26. Can I use the campus pool without a membership?  
Yes, for $5 per session. Students with swim certifications may access the pool at any time. Non-students require prior approval from the sports department and pay double the entry fee.

## 27. What safety protocols are in place for gym equipment?  
All machines have emergency stop buttons and weight limits displayed. Users must complete a brief orientation video before using advanced equipment. First aid stations are located near each fitness zone.

## 28. How do I schedule a group sports session?  
Contact the sports coordinator via email to propose your event. Provide details about the sport, number of participants, and preferred dates/times. The campus will confirm availability and provide necessary equipment.

## 29. What are the rules for using the campus bike racks?  
Bikes must be locked with a U-lock or similar device. Users are responsible for any damage caused by improper storage. Overflow racks are available near major buildings during peak hours.

## 30. How do I request a new parking spot?  
Submit a form through the transportation office detailing your needs. The campus will evaluate based on availability and demand. Approval typically takes six weeks, after which construction begins.

## 31. What is the procedure for reporting a security incident?  
Contact campus security immediately via phone or the emergency app. Provide details about the incident, including location, time, and any witnesses. A follow-up report will be sent to the relevant authorities within 24 hours.

## 32. Can I use my own printer in student housing?  
Yes, but only if it meets fire safety regulations. You’ll need to register your device with the building manager and pay a $50 installation fee. All prints must be supervised by staff during operating hours.

## 33. How do I access campus software from home?  
Use the virtual desktop interface available through the IT portal. This grants access to licensed software like Microsoft Office, Adobe Creative Suite, and specialized academic tools. A stable internet connection is required for optimal performance.

## 34. What are the rules for using the campus computer labs?  
Students may use lab computers for up to four hours daily. Personal devices must be registered in the IT system to access network resources. Lab hours are extended during exam periods to accommodate study needs.

## 35. How do I request a refund for unused meal plan credits?  
Submit a written request to the dining services office with proof of no remaining balances. Refunds are processed within two business days and issued via your student account. Unused credits cannot be transferred to future semesters.

## 36. Can I bring my own food into the cafeteria?  
Yes, but only in sealed containers. Personal items must not interfere with food safety or kitchen operations. Large quantities may require prior approval from the catering team.

## 37. What are the procedures for food waste disposal?  
All uneaten food must be disposed of in designated bins. Composting is encouraged for organic waste. Grease and oil should be poured into special containers to prevent clogging. Staff will monitor compliance during daily inspections.

## 38. How do I request a dietary accommodation at the cafeteria?  
Contact the dining services office with your specific requirements (e.g., halal, kosher, gluten-free). The campus will work with approved vendors to accommodate your needs. Documentation may be required for special requests.

## 39. What is the policy on using mobile devices in classrooms?  
Students are encouraged to use mobile devices for educational purposes but must keep them in silent mode during lectures. Devices should not disrupt learning or distract other students. Violations may result in confiscation until the end of class.

## 40. How do I report a faulty classroom projector?  
Use the maintenance request form in the facilities app. Include details about the issue and your contact information. The campus will schedule repairs within five business days. Emergency issues may receive priority treatment.

## 41. What are the guidelines for using the campus art studios?  
Studio hours are posted on the arts department website. Students must reserve time slots in advance and follow safety protocols for all equipment. No food or drinks are allowed in studio spaces to protect artwork and materials.

## 42. Can I use my own laptop in the library computer labs?  
Yes, but only if it meets the minimum system requirements (Windows 10 or macOS Catalina). Devices must be registered through the IT portal to access network resources. Laptops without registration may face connectivity issues.

## 43. How do I request a temporary extension for a late submission?  
Submit a formal request to your instructor with valid reasons (e.g., medical, family emergency). Extensions are granted at the instructor’s discretion and typically last up to seven days. Late submissions will still incur partial penalties unless approved by the faculty.

## 44. What is the procedure for requesting special accommodations for exams?  
Contact the disability services office with documentation of your condition. The campus will provide reasonable accommodations, which may include extended time, alternative formats, or modified testing environments. Requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## 45. How do I report a broken classroom whiteboard?  
Use the maintenance request form in the facilities app. Include photos of the damage and your contact information. The campus will schedule repairs within five business days. Emergency issues may receive priority treatment.

## 47. How do I request access to the campus computer labs during evenings?  
The computer labs are open until 10:00 PM on weekdays and 8:00 PM on weekends. To reserve a spot, use the facilities booking portal at least one day in advance. Priority is given to students with scheduled classes or study sessions.
## 48. What should I do if my device is not registered for campus software?  
Visit the IT service portal to complete the registration process. You'll need your student ID and a valid email address. Registration typically takes 24 hours, after which you can access licensed software like Microsoft Office and Adobe Creative Suite.
## 49. Can I use the campus printers for large print jobs?  
Yes, but there are limits on the size and number of pages. The maximum paper size is A3, and each job is limited to 50 pages. For larger prints, contact the printing services office to arrange a special order with a $10 fee per page.
## 50. How do I report a security incident involving lost or stolen property?  
Contact campus security immediately via phone or the emergency app. Provide details about the lost item, including its description and last known location. A follow-up report will be sent to the relevant authorities within 24 hours for further action.
