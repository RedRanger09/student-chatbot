# University Transport Guide

## Overview
The University of Eldoria provides a comprehensive transportation network to ensure students can commute safely and efficiently between campus locations, residential areas, and academic facilities. Our bus services operate seven days a week during the academic term, with extended hours during peak periods. This guide outlines all available transport options, registration procedures, fees, and student responsibilities to help you navigate our transportation system confidently.

## Bus Services
### College Bus Network
The university operates three main bus routes covering campus, dormitory areas, and peripheral academic buildings:

1. **Route 101 (Campus Loop)** - Serves the main campus, library, science center, and administrative offices
2. **Route 202 (Dormitory Link)** - Connects residence halls with academic buildings and student services
3. **Route 303 (Peripheral Access)** - Provides access to satellite labs, research facilities, and off-campus partner institutions

Each route runs every 15 minutes during peak hours (7:00 AM - 9:00 PM) and every 30 minutes during off-peak times. Buses are equipped with climate control, priority seating for seniors/Students with Disabilities, and real-time tracking via the university's mobile app.

### Special Services
- **Express Shuttle** - Rapid service between campus and major dormitory complexes (operates 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM)
- **Late-Night Route** - Extended service until 2:00 AM for students with late-night academic commitments
- **Weekend Service** - Reduced frequency on Saturdays (bi-hourly) and Sundays (hourly)

## Registration
### Transport Registration Process
All students must register for transport services through the Student Portal. Registration includes:

1. **Account Verification** - Confirm your student status via email verification
2. **Route Selection** - Choose preferred bus routes based on residence and class schedule
3. **Pass Application** - Apply for a reusable campus bus pass (valid for 10 academic terms)
4. **Emergency Contacts** - Provide emergency contact information for safety purposes

Registration is mandatory for all students requiring transport services. New students must complete registration within the first three weeks of enrollment.

### Bus Pass Application
To obtain a bus pass:
- Complete the online application form through the Student Portal
- Submit a copy of your student ID card
- Pay the annual transportation fee ($85 USD)
- Receive your pass via email as a digital QR code (printable version available on request)

Passes are valid for all university-operated buses and can be used for up to 10 academic terms. Renewal is automatic unless revoked for policy violations.

## Fees
### Transport Costs
The annual transportation fee includes:
- Base registration ($85 USD)
- Pass issuance ($20 USD)
- Emergency service access ($10 USD)

Students with disabilities may qualify for reduced fees through the Accessibility Services Office. Payment can be made via:
- Online portal (preferred method)
- Campus finance office
- Mobile payment apps

Refunds are available for incomplete terms at 75% of the annual fee, provided requested within two weeks of registration.

## Student Responsibilities
### Mandatory Compliance
Students must adhere to all transport policies:
1. **Pass Validity** - Use only valid passes with current expiration dates
2. **Route Adherence** - Ride only on registered routes; unauthorized route changes may result in pass suspension
3. **Behavior Standards** - Maintain respectful conduct toward staff and fellow passengers
4. **Safety Protocols** - Follow emergency evacuation procedures and report any safety concerns

### Reporting Issues
Students should immediately notify transportation services of:
- Lost or stolen passes (within 24 hours)
- Mechanical failures on buses
- Safety hazards in transport areas
- Discriminatory behavior by staff

All reports are handled confidentially and investigated within 72 hours.

## Frequently Encountered Situations
### Common Student Mistakes
1. **Expired Passes** - Over 30% of late arrivals result from expired passes; always check pass validity before boarding
2. **Incorrect Route Selection** - Choosing the wrong route may cause missed connections; use the Transport App for real-time route verification
3. **Luggage Restrictions** - Oversized items (over 18" x 15" x 10") are prohibited; use designated luggage storage areas

### Misunderstood Rules
- **Bus Pass Transferability** - Passes are personal and cannot be transferred to others without official authorization
- **Late Boarding Policy** - Students boarding after the bus leaves the stop may be charged a $5 fee for missed connections
- **Pet Policy** - Service animals are allowed with proper documentation; other pets are prohibited

### Emergency Procedures
In case of emergencies:
1. Evacuate to the nearest emergency exit
2. Use the bus's emergency intercom system
3. Contact campus security via the mobile app or 911
4. Follow staff instructions for shelter-in-place procedures

## Transport Complaints
Students can file complaints through:
- Online portal (preferred method)
- Physical counter at the Transportation Services Office
- Email to transport@eldoria.edu

Complaints must include:
- Date and time of incident
- Specific details of the issue
- Name of involved staff member (if known)

All complaints are reviewed within 14 business days and resolved through mediation if necessary.

## Emergency Transportation
For urgent situations:
- **Medical Emergencies** - Call 911 or use the campus security app for immediate assistance
- **Transportation Assistance** - Request special accommodations through Accessibility Services for students with mobility impairments
- **Lost Pass Recovery** - Report lost passes immediately to regain access via the emergency pass program
