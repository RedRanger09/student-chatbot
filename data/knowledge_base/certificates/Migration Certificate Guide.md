# Migration Certificate Guide

## Overview
A migration certificate is an official document issued by [University Name] that confirms a student's academic records and eligibility to transfer credits or enroll in courses at another institution. This certificate serves as proof of academic standing and facilitates seamless transition between educational institutions.

### Purpose
The migration certificate enables students to:
- Transfer academic credits between universities
- Apply for admission to new programs
- Verify enrollment status for international studies
- Maintain academic continuity during institutional transitions

## Eligibility
Students are eligible to request a migration certificate if:
1. They are currently enrolled as a full-time undergraduate or graduate student
2. They have completed at least one semester of coursework
3. They intend to transfer to another accredited institution
4. They meet the university's academic standing requirements (minimum GPA 2.0)

**Note:** International students must also demonstrate English language proficiency through TOEFL/IELTS scores.

## Application Process
### Online Request
1. Log in to [University Portal]
2. Navigate to "Academic Services" > "Migration Requests"
3. Select "Request Migration Certificate"
4. Complete the digital form with personal details
5. Upload supporting documents (see below)
6. Submit and pay processing fee

### Offline Request
Print and submit a completed paper application form from:
- Registrar's Office
- Academic Advising Center
- Student Affairs Department

## Processing
**Standard Processing Time:** 10 business days after document verification  
**Priority Service:** Available for $50 (processing time reduced to 5 days)

### Required Documents
- Completed application form
- Valid student ID
- Proof of enrollment (current transcript)
- Letter of good standing from current institution
- Payment receipt

## Verification
To verify a migration certificate:
1. Visit [University Verification Portal]
2. Enter certificate serial number
3. Confirm authenticity through institutional database
4. Download official verification report

**Note:** Verification is required for credit transfers and international enrollment.

## Duplicate Certificates
Duplicate certificates are issued for:
- Lost original documents
- Damage to existing certificate
- Requested by student or authorized representative

Processing fee: $30 for duplicates  
Verification process remains the same as original request

## Lost Certificates
If a migration certificate is lost:
1. Report loss to Registrar's Office immediately
2. Submit police report (if theft)
3. Provide proof of enrollment
4. Pay replacement fee ($50)
5. Wait 10 business days for new certificate

## Student Responsibilities
Students must:
- Maintain accurate academic records
- Report changes in enrollment status immediately
- Keep original certificate secure
- Notify university of any address changes
- Cooperate with verification requests

## Common Situations
### Scenario 1: Transfer Between Universities
A student transferring from [University A] to [University B] would need:
- Credit evaluation report
- Official transcript
- Migration certificate for credit validation
- Verification through both institutions' systems

### Scenario 2: International Study Abroad
Students studying abroad must:
- Obtain migration certificate before departure
- Carry original document with them
- Verify credentials with host institution
- Maintain academic records during absence

### Scenario 3: Academic Leave
Students on leave must:
- Request migration certificate for interim enrollment
- Provide proof of leave status
- Maintain contact information for verification
- Reapply upon return to campus

## Frequently Misunderstood Policies
1. **Certificate Validity:** Only valid for the academic term specified
2. **Transfer Equivalency:** Credits may not transfer directly - requires evaluation
3. **Verification Limits:** Only one verification per semester is allowed
4. **Fee Waivers:** Available for financial aid recipients (contact Registrar's Office)

## Migration Scenarios Examples
1. A student transferring from a community college to a four-year university needs a certificate to validate associate credits.
2. An international student moving to a new country requires a migration certificate for visa purposes.
3. A graduate student changing research focus may need a certificate for credit transfer between departments.
