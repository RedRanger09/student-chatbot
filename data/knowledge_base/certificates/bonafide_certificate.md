# Bonafide Certificate Guide

## Overview  
A **bonafide certificate** is an official document issued by [University of Eldoria](https://www.eldoria.edu) to verify a student's enrollment, academic standing, and compliance with university policies. This certificate serves as **proof of enrollment** and confirms that the student is actively pursuing their degree program without any disciplinary violations.  

### Purpose  
The **bonafide certificate** is required for:  
- Job applications (especially in government sectors)  
- Academic transfers between institutions  
- Verification of academic credentials by employers or other educational bodies  
- Legal documentation for immigration purposes  
- Participation in internships or research programs  

### Common Uses  
Students often need this certificate for:  
- **Certificate request** processes during campus recruitment drives  
- Proof of enrollment for scholarship applications  
- Verification of student status for international travel  
- Submission to professional licensing boards  

---

## Eligibility  
To be eligible for a **bonafide certificate**, students must:  
1. Be officially enrolled in a degree program at [University of Eldoria](https://www.eldoria.edu)  
2. Maintain a good academic standing (minimum 2.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale)  
3. Have no pending disciplinary actions or probationary status  
4. Submit a valid **certificate request** through the student portal  

**Note:** Graduate students must also provide proof of thesis submission approval.  

---

## Application Process  
### Required Documents  
- Valid ID proof (passport or Aadhaar card)  
- Completed application form  
- Proof of enrollment (if applicable)  
- Fee payment receipt  

### Online Application  
1. Log in to the [Student Portal](https://portal.eldoria.edu) using your credentials  
2. Navigate to "Academic Services" > "Bonafide Certificate Request"  
3. Fill out the form with:  
   - Full name  
   - Enrollment number  
   - Reason for **certificate request** (select from dropdown menu)  
   - Preferred delivery method (digital or physical)  
4. Upload a scanned copy of your ID proof  
5. Submit and pay the **application fee** of ₹300 via the portal's secure payment gateway  

### Offline Application  
For students without internet access:  
1. Visit the Registrar's Office at [Main Campus](https://www.eldoria.edu/main-campus) during business hours (Mon-Fri, 9 AM - 4 PM)  
2. Submit a handwritten application form with:  
   - Full name and enrollment number  
   - Reason for **certificate request**  
   - Photocopy of ID proof  
3. Pay the **application fee** in cash or via demand draft  

---

## Processing  
### Timeframe  
- **Online applications:** 5–7 working days after payment confirmation  
- **Offline applications:** 10–12 working days from submission date  

**Note:** Processing times may extend during examination periods (June–August) due to high volume.  

### Reissue Policy  
If a certificate is lost, damaged, or incorrect, students can request a reissue by:  
1. Submitting a formal written request to the Registrar's Office  
2. Paying a reissue fee of ₹200  
3. Providing proof of the original certificate's invalidity  

### Certificate Collection  
- **Digital copies:** Received via email and portal download link  
- **Physical copies:** Collected from the Registrar's Office with valid ID  

---

## Verification  
The certificate includes a unique QR code that links to the university's verification system. Employers or verifying bodies can scan this code to confirm:  
- Student's current enrollment status  
- Academic standing  
- No disciplinary violations  

---

## Common Mistakes  
### 1. Submitting an outdated certificate  
Always ensure your certificate is current before submitting it for any purpose.  

### 2. Missing required documents  
Forgetting to include ID proof or fee payment receipts can delay processing.  

### 3. Using forged documents  
Providing false information may result in cancellation of your certificate and disciplinary action.  

---

## Student Responsibilities  
1. Ensure all submitted information is accurate and up-to-date  
2. Pay the **application fee** on time to avoid delays  
3. Retain a copy of your **certificate request** for future reference  
4. Report any changes in academic status immediately to the Registrar's Office  
5. Avoid using forged documents—this may lead to cancellation of your certificate  

---

## Frequently Misunderstood Policies  
### 1. Can I use the same certificate for multiple purposes?  
No. Each **certificate request** must be submitted separately, as the document is tied to a specific application.  

### 2. What if my enrollment status changes after submission?  
If you're placed on probation or suspended, your certificate will be revoked. Always check your academic standing before applying.  

### 3. Is there a way to expedite processing?  
Yes. For an additional fee of ₹500, you can opt for **priority processing** (2–3 working days). This is ideal for urgent job applications.
