# Certificates FAQ

## 1. How do I apply for a bonafide certificate?  
To request a bonafide certificate, log in to your university portal and navigate to the "Certificate Requests" section. Select "Bonafide Certificate" from the dropdown menu. Fill out the form with your personal details, specify the purpose of the certificate (e.g., job application, visa process), and submit it for approval. Processing typically takes 5–7 business days. You can also request a physical copy via courier or download a digital version in PDF format.

## 2. How long does it take to receive my academic transcript?  
Transcript processing usually takes 3–5 business days after submission. During peak periods like graduation, delays of up to two weeks may occur. Ensure your payment is processed and all required documents are uploaded. For expedited service, select the "Priority Processing" option during application, which adds a $20 fee but reduces turnaround time to 1–3 days.

## 3. Can I request multiple transcript copies?  
Yes, you can request up to three official transcripts per semester for academic purposes. Each copy incurs a separate fee of $15. For bulk requests (four or more), contact the Registrar’s Office for discounted rates. Note that third-party institutions may require sealed envelopes with official stamps for authenticity.

## 4. What documents are needed for a migration certificate?  
To apply for a migration certificate, submit your original degree certificate, a letter from your current institution confirming enrollment, and proof of financial stability (e.g., bank statements). International applicants must also provide an attested translation of all documents in English. Applications submitted with incomplete paperwork will be delayed by at least 10 business days.

## 5. When can I apply for a provisional degree?  
You may apply for a provisional degree once you’ve completed 75% of your required coursework and have no pending academic sanctions. Submit the request through the "Provisional Degree" portal, which opens three months before graduation. Approval is typically granted within two weeks, but delays can occur during final exams.

## 6. Can someone else collect my certificate?  
Yes, a third party may collect your certificate if they present a notarized letter of authorization from you and valid photo ID. The representative must also provide their own ID for verification. This process is only available for physical certificates; digital versions cannot be collected by proxy.

## 7. What should I do if my certificate contains incorrect information?  
Immediately contact the Registrar’s Office via email or phone to report errors. Provide copies of your original document and any supporting evidence (e.g., official grade reports). Corrections take 5–10 business days, during which a temporary replacement will be issued. Repeat offenses may result in a $50 fee for verification.

## 8. Can I receive my certificate by courier?  
Yes, all physical certificates can be delivered via registered post to your provided address. Courier charges vary based on location: local deliveries cost $10, national shipments $25, and international mail $75. Track your package using the tracking number sent via email after payment confirmation.

## 9. How do employers verify my certificate?  
Employers can request a digital verification through the university’s official portal by entering your name and ID number. Alternatively, they may contact the Registrar’s Office directly with your details for confirmation. Always ensure your certificate includes an official seal and tamper-evident watermark.

## 10. What happens if I lose my original certificate?  
Report the loss immediately to the Registrar’s Office via email or phone. Submit a police report (if applicable) and fill out a duplicate request form. A $30 fee applies for replacement, which includes a new physical copy and digital version. Keep your duplicate safe, as it is legally equivalent to the original.

## 1
What are the requirements for obtaining a digital certificate?  
To receive a digital certificate, you must first submit an online application through the university’s portal. Provide a scanned copy of your ID and proof of enrollment. Once approved, download the PDF version from your account. Digital certificates cannot be printed or shared with third parties without prior approval from the Registrar’s Office.

## 2
How do I correct my name on a certificate?  
Request a name correction by submitting a formal letter to the Registrar’s Office along with official documentation (e.g., birth certificate, marriage license). The correction process takes 7–10 business days. Note that corrections are only allowed for legal name changes or typos; full name updates may require reissuance of all certificates.

## 3
Can I request a certificate for a course I dropped?  
No, certificates are only issued for completed courses listed on your academic transcript. If you withdrew from a course after the drop deadline, it will appear as "Withdrawn" and not be included in official records. Contact the Registrar’s Office to confirm your current enrollment status.

## 4
What is the validity period of a certificate?  
All certificates are valid indefinitely for verification purposes but should be used within 10 years of issuance for employment or immigration applications. Expired certificates may be rejected by employers or institutions, so always check with the recipient’s requirements before submission.

## 5
How do I track the status of my certificate request?  
Check your application status via the university portal under "Certificate Requests." You can also call the Registrar’s Office directly using the contact number listed on your student handbook. Updates are typically posted within 24 hours of processing.

## 6
What are the fees for international certificate requests?  
International applicants pay an additional $50 fee for expedited shipping and currency conversion. Payments must be made in USD via bank transfer or credit card. Allow 10–15 business days for delivery to remote locations, with tracking updates sent weekly.

## 7
Can I get a certificate if I’m currently on academic probation?  
No, certificates are only issued to students in good standing. Probationary status may delay your request by up to two months while you resolve academic issues. Contact the Academic Advising Office for guidance on reinstatement requirements.

## 8
What should I do if my certificate is damaged or lost abroad?  
Submit a written report to the Registrar’s Office detailing the incident. Provide any available evidence (e.g., photos, police reports). A replacement will be issued within 10 business days, with shipping costs covered for international applicants. Keep your duplicate safe during travel.

## 9
Are there any restrictions on using digital certificates?  
Digital certificates cannot be printed or altered without prior approval. They are only valid for official verification through the university’s portal. Employers requesting physical copies must submit a formal letter of request, which will trigger a reissuance process.

## 10
How do I apply for a certificate of graduation clearance?  
Submit a request through the "Graduation Clearance" section of your student portal. Provide proof of completed coursework and any outstanding financial obligations. Approval takes 5–7 business days, after which you’ll receive an email confirming eligibility for official documents.
