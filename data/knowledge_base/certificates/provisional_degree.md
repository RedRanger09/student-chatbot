# Provisional Degree Guide

## Overview

A provisional degree certificate is a temporary document issued to graduating students before their official degree is finalized. This certificate serves as proof of academic completion and allows students to proceed with post-graduation formalities such as job applications, higher education admissions, and professional certifications. It is distinct from the final degree certificate, which is officially recorded in the university's academic records after all administrative processes are complete.

The provisional degree is designed to ensure that students can access critical services and opportunities without delay while their official degree is being processed. This guide outlines the eligibility criteria, application process, processing timelines, verification procedures, and other essential information for obtaining a provisional degree certificate.

---

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a provisional degree certificate, students must meet the following criteria:

1. **Academic Completion**: Successfully complete all required coursework and achieve the minimum grade point average (GPA) specified by the university.
2. **Graduation Clearance**: Receive confirmation that all financial obligations, including tuition fees and library fines, are settled.
3. **No Pending Disciplinary Actions**: Have no unresolved disciplinary cases or academic misconduct allegations.
4. **Submission of Required Documents**: Provide all necessary documentation, such as transcripts, degree application forms, and proof of identity.

Students who meet these criteria are automatically eligible to apply for a provisional degree certificate upon completing their final exams.

---

## Application Process

### Step 1: Submit Graduation Application
Students must first submit their graduation application through the university's online portal. This includes selecting the desired degree program and confirming all academic requirements have been met.

### Step 2: Verify Academic Records
The university's registrar office will verify the student's academic records to ensure all coursework has been completed and grades are satisfactory. Any discrepancies or missing documents may delay the issuance of the provisional certificate.

### Step 3: Pay Application Fees
A non-refundable application fee is required to process the provisional degree request. Payment can be made online via the university's payment portal or in person at the finance office.

### Step 4: Submit Additional Documentation
Students may need to submit additional documentation, such as a letter of recommendation or proof of internship completion, depending on their program requirements.

### Step 5: Wait for Approval
Once all documents are verified and fees are paid, the registrar's office will review the application. Students will receive an email notification confirming whether their application has been approved.

---

## Processing

The provisional degree certificate is typically issued within **10 business days** of receiving a complete application. During this period, the university ensures that all academic records are accurate and that there are no outstanding obligations.

### Certificate Validity
A provisional degree certificate is valid for **one year** from the date of issuance. Students must apply for an official degree certificate before this validity period expires to avoid any disruptions in their post-graduation activities.

---

## Verification

To verify the authenticity of a provisional degree certificate, follow these steps:

1. **Check the University's Official Website**: Visit the university's official website and use the online verification tool to confirm the certificate's validity.
2. **Contact the Registrar's Office**: Reach out to the registrar's office directly for manual verification if needed.
3. **Use Third-Party Verification Services**: Some employers and institutions offer third-party services to verify academic credentials.

It is important to note that provisional certificates are not considered official records until the final degree certificate is issued.

---

## Frequently Encountered Situations

### 1. When is a Provisional Degree Needed?
Students may need a provisional degree certificate in the following scenarios:
- Applying for jobs or internships immediately after graduation.
- Enrolling in graduate programs that require proof of academic completion.
- Participating in professional certification exams that require official documentation.

### 2. Difference Between Provisional and Final Degree Certificates
| Feature               | Provisional Degree Certificate       | Final Degree Certificate         |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Validity**          | Valid for one year                   | Permanent                        |
| **Issuance Time**     | Issued before final degree           | Issued after all administrative processes |
| **Verification**      | Can be verified through online tools | Official record in university archives |

### 3. Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Missing Documents**: Ensure all required documents are submitted with the application.
- **Late Fees**: Pay the application fee on time to avoid delays.
- **Incorrect Information**: Provide accurate personal and academic details to prevent verification issues.

---

## Student Responsibilities

Students are responsible for:
- Ensuring all academic requirements are met before applying for a provisional degree.
- Submitting complete and accurate documentation.
- Paying all required fees promptly.
- Keeping track of the certificate's validity period and applying for the official degree certificate before it expires.

By fulfilling these responsibilities, students can ensure a smooth transition into post-graduation opportunities while their official degree is being processed.
