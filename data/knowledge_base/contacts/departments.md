## Contact Summary

### Common Reasons to Contact Departments
- **Admissions Office**: Application queries, fee payment issues  
- **Registrar's Office**: Transcript requests, degree verification  
- **Examination Cell**: Result inquiries, exam corrections  
- **Finance Office**: Payment processing, budget-related questions  
- **Scholarship Office**: Scholarship applications, financial aid counseling  
- **Hostel Office**: Room allocation, facility requests  
- **Placement Cell**: Job placements, interview preparation  
- **Library**: Resource access, research assistance  
- **Student Welfare Office**: Mental health support, crisis intervention  
- **IT Help Desk**: Technical support, software installation  

### Best Contact Methods
- **Email** for non-urgent academic and administrative inquiries  
- **Phone** for immediate support or urgent issues  
- **In-person visits** for complex matters requiring detailed discussion  

### Response Expectations
- **Email responses** within 24 hours (business days)  
- **Phone calls** answered promptly during office hours  
- **In-person appointments** scheduled in advance for specialized services
