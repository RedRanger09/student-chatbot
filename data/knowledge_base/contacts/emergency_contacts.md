## Emergency Guidelines

### When to Use Each Contact
- **Campus Security:** For thefts, suspicious activities, or security breaches  
- **Medical Emergency:** For critical injuries, heart attacks, or severe bleeding  
- **Ambulance:** For transport to hospitals during medical emergencies  
- **Fire Response:** For fires in dormitories or laboratories  
- **Women's Safety Helpline:** For harassment, abuse, or domestic violence cases  
- **Anti-Ragging Helpline:** For incidents of bullying or hazing in hostels  
- **Hostel Wardens:** For disciplinary issues or welfare concerns in dorms  
- **IT Support:** For network outages, software glitches, or device malfunctions  
- **Power & Maintenance:** For electrical failures or infrastructure repairs  
- **Counseling:** For mental health crises, stress management, or academic pressure  

### Emergency Reporting Tips
1. Stay calm and provide clear details of the incident.  
2. Use the nearest emergency phone number for quick response.  
3. Avoid using non-emergency lines for critical situations.  
4. Inform authorities about the location, nature of the incident, and casualties if any.  
5. Follow evacuation protocols in case of fires or lockdowns.  

### Important Reminders
- Always dial 112 for police emergencies.  
- Keep emergency numbers saved in your mobile phone contacts.  
- Familiarize yourself with campus safety routes and nearest exits.  
- Report any suspicious activity to Campus Security immediately.  
- Use the Women's Safety Helpline if you feel threatened or harassed.  
- In case of a medical emergency, prioritize calling 108 for ambulance services.
