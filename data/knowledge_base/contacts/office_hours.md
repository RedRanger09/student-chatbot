## Student Support Services

### Online Support Availability
- **Email Support:** Available 24/7 for non-urgent queries
- **Live Chat:** Operational from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Monday to Friday
- **Help Desk Portal:** Accessible via university portal for ticket submission and status tracking

### Extended Hours During Admission and Examination Periods
- **Admission Period (August 1 - August 31):** All offices operate from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM
- **Examination Period (December 1 - December 31):** Offices extend hours to 8:00 PM

### Holiday Schedule Overview
- **Public Holidays:** 
  - Independence Day (July 4)
  - Christmas Day (December 25)
  - New Year's Day (January 1)
  - Republic Day (January 14)
  - Good Friday (April 9)
  - Eid al-Fitr (May 17)
  - Diwali (October 30)

### Semester Break Timings
- **Semester Break:** July 15 - August 15
- **Winter Break:** December 20 - January 10

## Important Notes
- Always confirm appointment details via the university portal or email.
- Emergency inquiries outside regular hours should be directed to the IT Help Desk or Health Center emergency contacts.
- The library and examination cell maintain extended hours during peak academic periods.
- Students are encouraged to plan visits during off-peak hours to minimize wait times.
