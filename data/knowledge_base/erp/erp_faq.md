# ERP FAQ

## 1. How do I log in to the ERP portal?  
Access the ERP portal by visiting the official university website and navigating to the student login section. Enter your registered email address and password. If you're a first-time user, use your enrollment ID as the username. Click "Login" to proceed. Ensure you’re using a supported browser and a stable internet connection for a smooth experience.

## 2. What should I do if I can't access the ERP portal?  
Check your internet connection and try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, ensure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge). Clear your browser cache and cookies. If problems continue, verify your login credentials or contact the IT helpdesk for assistance.

## 3. How do I reset my ERP password if I forgot it?  
Visit the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your registered email address or enrollment ID. Follow the prompts to receive a password reset link via email. If you don’t receive it, check your spam folder or contact support for manual intervention.

## 4. What steps should I take if my ERP account is locked?  
A locked account typically occurs after multiple failed login attempts. Wait 24 hours for automatic unlocking. If not resolved, visit the "Account Recovery" section on the portal homepage and submit a request with your enrollment details. IT will review and unlock your account.

## 5. How can I retrieve my ERP username if I don’t remember it?  
Your ERP username is usually your enrollment ID or the email address used during registration. Check your admission confirmation email or contact the registrar’s office for verification. If unsure, use the "Username Recovery" tool on the login page with your personal details.

## 6. What should I do if my ERP password is incorrect?  
Double-check your password for typos, ensuring case sensitivity and special characters are included. If you’re still unable to log in, try resetting your password via the "Forgot Password" link. Avoid sharing your credentials with anyone to prevent unauthorized access.

## 7. How do I update my mobile number in the ERP portal?  
Log in to your account, navigate to the "Profile Settings" section, and edit your contact information. Save changes after verifying your current password. Ensure your new number is correctly formatted to avoid delivery issues.

## 8. Why does my ERP session expire automatically?  
ERP sessions timeout after 30 minutes of inactivity to enhance security. Keep your browser open during prolonged use or log in again if interrupted. Avoid closing the tab mid-task to prevent data loss.

## 9. How do I check my attendance records on the ERP portal?  
Log in and navigate to the "Attendance" section under your dashboard. Select your semester and course details to view real-time updates. Print or download reports for record-keeping, ensuring accuracy before final submissions.

## 10. What should I do if my marks are not visible on the portal?  
Marks typically update within 72 hours of grading completion. Check the "Results" section under your dashboard for your latest grades. If delays persist, contact your department head or the academic office for clarification.

## 11. How can I view my academic timetable?  
Access your timetable via the "Academic Calendar" tab on the portal homepage. Filter by semester and course code to see class schedules, room numbers, and instructor details. Download PDF versions for offline reference.

## 12. What steps should I follow to register for new courses?  
Log in and navigate to the "Course Registration" section. Review available electives and prerequisites before selecting courses. Submit your choices by the deadline, ensuring all requirements are met to avoid registration conflicts.

## 13. How do I pay my semester fees online via the ERP portal?  
Access the "Fee Payment" section, select your payment method (net banking, UPI, or card), and complete the transaction. Save your receipt for future reference. Avoid late payments to avoid additional charges.

## 14. Where can I find my fee payment receipts?  
Navigate to the "Transactions" tab under your account settings. Filter by "Fee Payment" to view all receipts. Download or print them for record-keeping, ensuring they match your bank statements.

## 15. How do I register for examinations?  
Visit the "Examination Registration" section on the portal homepage. Select your exam dates and subjects, then confirm your choices. Ensure you’ve met all prerequisites to avoid disqualification.

## 16. What should I do if my admit card is not generated?  
Check your email for a confirmation message or visit the "Admit Card" section under your dashboard. If missing, contact the examination office with your enrollment details for manual generation and verification.

## 17. How can I download my admit card?  
Log in to your account, navigate to the "Examinations" tab, and select "Download Admit Card." Save the PDF file securely. Print a hard copy for on-site use, ensuring all personal details are accurate.

## 18. Where can I find the academic calendar for upcoming events?  
Access the "Academic Calendar" section on your dashboard. Filter by semester to view key dates like registration deadlines, exam schedules, and holiday breaks. Mark these dates in your planner to stay organized.

## 19. How do I apply for scholarships through the ERP portal?  
Navigate to the "Scholarship Portal" section and select applicable programs. Submit your application with required documents like transcripts and recommendation letters. Track your status via the "My Applications" tab for updates.

## 20. What steps should I take to access the library account?  
Log in to the ERP portal and navigate to the "Library Services" section. Use your enrollment ID to retrieve book reservations, fines, or overdue notices. Renew items online to avoid penalties.

## 21. How do I receive notifications about my academic progress?  
Enable email alerts in your profile settings under "Notification Preferences." Choose updates like grade releases, exam schedules, and course changes. Verify your contact details regularly to ensure timely communication.

## 22. Can I access the ERP portal on my mobile device?  
Yes! Use the official university app or a mobile-friendly browser to access the portal. Ensure you’re logged in securely and avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive transactions. Download the app from the App Store or Google Play.

## 23. What should I do if the ERP portal is down or unavailable?  
Check the official social media channels or website for service status updates. If issues persist, try accessing the portal during off-peak hours. Contact IT support for urgent assistance if critical tasks are delayed.

## 24. How can I resolve browser compatibility issues with the ERP portal?  
Use Chrome, Firefox, or Edge as recommended browsers. Disable pop-up blockers and ad extensions temporarily. Clear cache and cookies, then refresh the page. If problems persist, contact technical support for further troubleshooting.

## 25. What are the steps to report a technical issue with the ERP portal?  
Navigate to the "Technical Support" section on the homepage. Submit a detailed description of the problem, including error messages and screenshots if possible. IT will prioritize your request based on severity and provide resolution timelines.

## 26. How do I update my profile information in the ERP system?

## 27. What should I do if my session times out while working on a form?

## 28. How can I check if the ERP portal is available during holidays?
