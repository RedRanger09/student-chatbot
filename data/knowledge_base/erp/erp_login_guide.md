# ERP Login Guide

## Overview
The University ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system is a centralized platform that provides students with access to academic resources, financial information, course registrations, and other essential university services. This guide explains how to log in to the ERP portal, navigate its dashboard, and troubleshoot common login issues.

### What You'll Find Here
- Step-by-step instructions for your first-time login
- Detailed guidance on accessing your account
- Troubleshooting solutions for login problems
- Security best practices to protect your account
- Common mistakes students make and how to avoid them

## First-Time Login
1. **Create Your ERP Account**  
   - Visit [https://erp.university.edu](https://erp.university.edu) and click "New Student Registration"
   - Enter your personal details (name, date of birth, contact information)
   - Select your program of study and campus location
   - Confirm your enrollment via the verification email sent to your university-provided address

2. **Receive Initial Credentials**  
   - Your temporary username will be your student ID number (e.g., `STU123456`)
   - A temporary password will be emailed to you within 24 hours of registration
   - Keep this information secure—you'll need it for future logins

3. **Set Up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**  
   - MFA is optional but strongly recommended for account security
   - Choose between:
     - SMS-based verification (via university-provided mobile number)
     - Authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator)
     - Security key (hardware token)

## Login Process
### Step 1: Access the Portal
1. Open your preferred web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
2. Navigate to [https://erp.university.edu](https://erp.university.edu)
3. Click "Student Login" in the top-right corner

### Step 2: Enter Credentials
1. Type your **student ID number** as your username
2. Enter your **password** (note: passwords are case-sensitive)
3. If MFA is enabled, enter the verification code from your chosen method

### Step 3: Access Your Dashboard
- After a successful login, you'll be redirected to your ERP dashboard
- The dashboard provides:
  - Course schedules and syllabi
  - Financial aid and payment history
  - Academic performance reports
  - Library resources and reserves
  - Student service portals (career center, counseling, etc.)

### Step 4: Remember Your Credentials
- Save your password in a secure password manager
- Avoid sharing your credentials with anyone
- Change your password every 90 days

## Troubleshooting
### Common Login Issues
| Problem | Solution |
|--------|---------|
| "Invalid username or password" | Double-check your student ID and password. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password?" link. |
| "Account locked" | Contact the IT Help Desk at helpdesk@university.edu or call 123-456-7890. Your account will be unlocked after verification. |
| "Session timeout" | Log out of all devices and try again. Sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. |

## Security Tips
1. **Use Strong Passwords**  
   - Include a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters
   - Avoid using common words or phrases (e.g., "password123")
   - Consider using a password manager like Bitwarden or 1Password

2. **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication**  
   - MFA adds an extra layer of security by requiring verification through multiple methods
   - Even if someone guesses your password, they'll still need your phone or security key to access your account

3. **Be Cautious with Links and Attachments**  
   - Never click on suspicious links in emails or messages
   - Verify the sender's email address before clicking any links
   - Avoid downloading attachments from unknown sources

4. **Regularly Update Your Browser**  
   - Outdated browsers may contain security vulnerabilities
   - Enable automatic updates for your web browser

5. **Log Out After Use**  
   - Always log out of the ERP portal when using public or shared computers
   - Click "Logout" in the top-right corner to end your session

## Frequently Encountered Problems
### Problem 1: Forgotten Password
- **Solution**: Use the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page. You'll receive instructions via email to reset your password.

### Problem 2: Incorrect Username
- **Solution**: Your username is your student ID number (e.g., `STU123456`). If you're unsure, contact the Registrar's Office at registrar@university.edu.

### Problem 3: MFA Verification Failure
- **Solution**: Ensure you've entered the correct verification code. If using an authenticator app, check that it's synchronized with your account. If issues persist, reset your MFA settings through the account settings menu.

### Problem 4: Session Timeout During Use
- **Solution**: The ERP system automatically logs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity. Save your work frequently and log back in if needed.

### Problem 5: Login Page Not Loading
- **Solution**: Try clearing your browser cache and cookies. If the issue persists, try a different browser or contact IT support.

## Student Responsibilities
- Protect your account credentials and do not share them with anyone
- Report any suspicious activity to the university's security team immediately
- Keep your personal information up-to-date in the ERP system
- Familiarize yourself with the university's privacy policies and data protection guidelines
