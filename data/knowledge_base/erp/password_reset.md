# ERP Password Reset Guide

## Overview
This guide explains how students can recover access to their university's Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) account. The ERP system manages critical academic and administrative functions, including course registration, financial aid, and student services. If you've forgotten your password or encountered login issues, follow these steps to regain access securely.

---

## Password Recovery

### Forgotten Password
If you've forgotten your ERP password, use the **Password Reset** feature to recover access. This process requires identity verification through one of the following methods:

1. **Email Verification**: Confirm your university email address is associated with your account.
2. **Mobile Verification**: Verify ownership of your registered mobile number.
3. **Security Questions**: Answer pre-set security questions (if enabled).

> ⚠️ *Note: If you're unable to verify your identity through these methods, contact the IT Support Desk for further assistance.*

### Reset Process
1. Navigate to the ERP login page.
2. Click **"Forgot Password?"** or **"Reset Password"**.
3. Select your preferred verification method (email/mobile/security questions).
4. Follow prompts to complete verification.
5. Set a new password that meets the system's requirements.

---

## Verification

### Email Verification
- **Step 1**: Enter your registered university email address.
- **Step 2**: A reset link will be sent to your inbox. Check your spam/junk folder if it doesn't appear immediately.
- **Step 3**: Click the link and follow instructions to create a new password.

> 📩 *Example Scenario*:  
A student named Alex forgot their ERP password. They clicked "Forgot Password," selected email verification, and received a reset link at `student@university.edu`. After clicking the link, they created a temporary password and logged in successfully.

### Mobile Verification
- **Step 1**: Enter your registered mobile number.
- **Step 2**: A code will be sent via SMS. Enter the code to proceed.
- **Step 3**: Create a new password and confirm it matches your account's requirements.

> 📱 *Common Issue*: If you don't receive the code, check for network issues or try resending the verification request.

---

## Password Requirements
Your ERP password must meet these criteria to ensure security:
- Minimum length: 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
- At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- No spaces or tabs allowed
- Must not contain your username, email, or personal information

> 🔒 *Tip*: Use a password manager to generate and store complex passwords securely.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Reset Problems
1. **Verification Failure**:  
   - Ensure you're using the correct registered email/mobile number.
   - Check for typos in your input.
   - Wait 5 minutes before retrying (some systems have rate limits).

2. **Locked Account**  
   - Multiple failed login attempts may trigger an account lockout.  
   - Contact IT Support to unlock your account and reset the password.

3. **Temporary Password Issues**  
   - Temporary passwords expire after 24 hours. Log in immediately after receiving one.
   - If you lose the temporary password, initiate a new reset process.

### Frequently Misunderstood Policies
- **Password Expiration**: ERP passwords expire every 90 days. You must change your password before expiration to avoid login issues.
- **Account Recovery**: If you believe someone has accessed your account, report it immediately via the IT Help Desk portal.
- **Security Questions**: These are used only for account recovery and not stored in plaintext.

---

## Security Recommendations
1. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: Use an authenticator app or hardware token for added security.
2. **Avoid Password Reuse**: Never use the same password across multiple accounts.
3. **Monitor Account Activity**: Regularly check your ERP account for unusual activity.
4. **Use Strong, Unique Passwords**: Follow the requirements outlined above to minimize breach risks.

> 🛡️ *Example*: A student named Jamie used a temporary password "TempPass123!" and changed it to a stronger password after logging in. This practice protects against unauthorized access.
