# ERP Portal Features

## Dashboard
The student dashboard is your centralized hub for accessing all essential information and services. It provides a quick overview of your academic progress, upcoming deadlines, and personalized notifications. Key elements include:
- **Quick Links:** Direct access to course registration, fee payments, and examination schedules.
- **Notifications:** Real-time updates on assignment submissions, event reminders, and system announcements.
- **Personalized Metrics:** Visual indicators for attendance rates, academic performance trends, and financial status.

**Navigation Tip:** Use the dashboard to monitor your progress without diving into individual sections. The "My Tasks" widget highlights pending actions like submission deadlines or required document uploads.

## Academic Features
### Course Registration
Students can browse available courses, check prerequisites, and enroll in classes during designated registration periods. The system automatically suggests compatible electives based on your academic plan.

**Workflow Example:**  
1. Access the "Course Catalog" from the dashboard  
2. Filter by department/year/semester  
3. Select desired courses and submit registration

### Internal Marks
Your progress in each course is tracked through regular assessments, quizzes, and assignments. The portal displays:
- Detailed grade breakdowns by module  
- Comparative analysis with class averages  
- Feedback from instructors on submitted work  

**Common Mistake:** Don't wait until the last minute to check marks - use the "Performance Analytics" tool to identify areas needing improvement.

### Semester Results
Final results are published in a structured format showing:
- Grade points for each course  
- GPA calculation  
- Academic standing (e.g., Cum Laude, Honor Roll)  

**Retrieval Optimization:** Use keywords like "semester results" or "academic performance" to quickly locate this information.

## Financial Features
### Fee Payment
The portal streamlines tuition and miscellaneous fee payments through:
- Multiple payment gateway options (credit card, bank transfer, etc.)  
- Invoice generation with breakdown of charges  
- Receipt download after successful transactions  

**Security Note:** Always verify payment confirmations via email notifications. The system automatically flags duplicate transactions.

### Fee Receipts
Digital receipts are available for all financial transactions. Students can:
- Download PDF copies for record-keeping  
- Share receipts via email  
- Export data for accounting purposes  

**Tip:** Use the "Payment History" filter to quickly locate specific transactions by date or amount.

## Examination Features
### Examination Registration
Students must register for exams through the portal, specifying:
- Preferred exam center locations  
- Special accommodations (if applicable)  
- Request for scribe/extra time (for disabled students)  

**Deadline Reminder:** Registration closes 7 days before the first scheduled exam date.

### Admit Card Download
After successful registration, students can:
- View and print admit cards  
- Check exam rules and guidelines  
- Verify personal details on the card  

**Common Mistake:** Don't use faded or damaged prints - always download a fresh copy from the portal.

## Student Services
### Hostel Information
The portal provides:
- Room allocation schedules  
- Maintenance request forms  
- Emergency contact protocols  
- Facility usage policies (e.g., laundry hours)  

**Navigation Tip:** Use the "Hostel Portal" link in the main menu for quick access to housing-related information.

### Library Account
Students can manage their library accounts through:
- Book reservations and renewals  
- Fines calculation and payment  
- Request for inter-library loans  
- Tracking of borrowed materials  

**Feature Highlight:** The "Reading History" section helps identify overdue books and late fees.

## Tips for Using the Portal
1. **Regular Updates:** Log in daily to check for new notifications and updates to your schedule.
2. **Password Security:** Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication for sensitive functions.
3. **Search Optimization:** Use specific keywords like "admit card download" or "fee payment history" for faster results.
4. **Help Desk Access:** The portal includes a chatbot for common queries and links to contact support staff.

**Common Mistake Alert:** Avoid using public computers for financial transactions - always clear your browsing history after use.
