# Examination FAQ

## 1. What happens if I forget my admit card?
If you forget your admit card, you must contact the examination office immediately. They will verify your details and issue a temporary permit. Ensure you carry it to avoid delays.

## 2. Can I enter the exam hall late?
Late entry is allowed only within 15 minutes of the scheduled start time. Show valid ID and a note from the proctor for approval.

## 3. How do supplementary exams work?
Supplementary exams are offered to students who failed regular exams. Register via the portal, pay fees, and attend designated dates.

## 4. Can I use a scientific calculator?
Yes, but only non-programmable models approved by the university. Check the exam guidelines for permitted brands.

## 5. What if my phone rings during the test?
Phones must be switched off and stored in sealed bags. Unauthorized use may lead to malpractice charges.

## 6. How is attendance eligibility determined?
Attendance is tracked via biometric systems. Minimum 75% attendance required for exam eligibility, with exceptions for medical emergencies.

## 7. What should I do if my answer booklet is damaged?
Inform the invigilator immediately. A replacement will be provided, but marks for any torn pages may be deducted.

## 8. Can I leave the exam hall early?
Yes, but you must submit your answer sheet and seek permission from the proctor. Leave without approval risks disqualification.

## 9. What are the rules for rough work?
Rough work must be done in designated areas. Using exam paper for calculations is prohibited; only pre-allocated sheets are allowed.

## 10. How do I apply for revaluation?
Submit a formal request within 15 days of results via the online portal. Pay fees and provide evidence of discrepancies.

## 11. What happens if I'm caught cheating?
Cheating leads to immediate disqualification. Penalties include academic suspension and potential legal action under university regulations.

## 12. Can I carry my phone switched off?
Phones must be stored in sealed bags. Even switched-off devices may be confiscated for security checks.

## 13. What is the grace period for late entry?
Late entry is permitted only within 15 minutes of start time. Proctors will not admit candidates after this window.

## 14. How are practical exams conducted?
Practical exams assess hands-on skills via lab sessions. Follow safety protocols and submit written reports as specified in the syllabus.

## 15. What should I do during a medical emergency?
Inform invigilators immediately. Medical staff will attend to you, but exam time may be reduced proportionally.

## 16. Can I use smart watches during exams?
No. Wearables must be removed and stored. Unauthorized devices may trigger security alerts.

## 17. What are the identity verification procedures?
Show ID matching your admit card. Biometric scans or fingerprint checks may be used for verification.

## 18. How do I request special accommodations?
Submit a formal application with medical proof to the disabilities office at least two weeks before exams.

## 19. What if I miss an exam due to unavoidable circumstances?
Provide documented evidence (medical certificates, travel logs) to the examination committee for possible rescheduling.

## 20. How are results published?
Results are uploaded to the portal within 15 days. Check your profile regularly and download official certificates.

## 21. What is the grace mark policy?
Grace marks up to 5% may be awarded for minor errors, but this does not guarantee passing. Focus on meeting minimum criteria.

## 22. Can I use multiple calculators?
No. Only one non-programmable calculator per student is allowed. Extra devices will be seized.

## 23. What if my admit card has an error?
Contact the examination office immediately. They will issue a corrected copy, but you must present it for entry.

## 24. How are exam schedules determined?
Schedules are based on course loads and faculty availability. Check your portal regularly for updates.

## 25. What should I do if I lose my admit card?
Report to the examination office immediately. A temporary permit may be issued, but you must carry it until a replacement arrives.

## 26. Can I bring stationery items into the exam hall?
Yes, but only basic supplies like pencils, erasers, and rulers. Electronic gadgets or extra paper are prohibited.

## 27. What happens if I arrive after reporting time?
You will not be allowed entry. Ensure you reach the hall at least 30 minutes before scheduled start times.

## 28. How do I handle exam-related queries during the test?
Raise your hand and wait for invigilator assistance. Do not communicate with other candidates or seek help discreetly.

## 29. What are the rules for answer sheet formatting?
Use black ink for all answers. Scribbles, corrections, or unauthorized markings may lead to score deductions.

## 30. Can I use correction fluid on answer sheets?
No. All corrections must be made with a pen. Tape or glue is not permitted for attaching extra pages.

## 31. What if I need to leave temporarily during the exam?
Inform invigilators immediately. Leave only with their permission, and return within the allotted time frame.

## 32. How are exams conducted for visually impaired students?
Special arrangements include Braille materials, audio aids, and extended time. Apply for these through the disabilities office.

## 33. What should I do if I find a mistake in my answer sheet?
Inform invigilators immediately. They will note corrections, but no changes can be made after submission.

## 34. Can I use colored pens for answering questions?
Only black or blue ink is permitted. Colored pens may lead to automatic disqualification for irregularities.

## 35. What are the consequences of unauthorized late entry?
You will be disqualified. No exceptions are made for arriving after the designated cutoff time.

## 36. How do I apply for a medical certificate for exam absences?
Contact the health center and submit documentation within seven days of the missed exam date.

## 37. What if my admit card is not in my name?
Do not enter the hall. Contact the examination office immediately for resolution before entry is permitted.

## 38. Can I use a watch during exams?
Yes, but only analog watches without calculators or alarms. Digital devices must be stored securely.

## 39. What happens if I submit incomplete answers?
Marks will be awarded based on what's submitted. Ensure all sections are completed before time runs out.

## 40. How do I handle exam questions that seem unfair?
Focus on answering based on syllabus content. Disputes over question validity are resolved post-exam through formal channels.

## 41. What should I do if my seat is not assigned properly?
Inform the proctor immediately. Seat adjustments will be made, but you must remain in your assigned area once seated.

## 42. Can I request a break during the exam?
No. Exams are conducted continuously unless specified otherwise in the guidelines. Plan accordingly for long sessions.

## 43. What if I need to use the restroom during the test?
Raise your hand and wait for permission. Leave only with invigilator approval, and return promptly to avoid penalties.

## 44. How are exam results reviewed for errors?
Submit a revaluation request within 15 days of publication. The process involves checking calculations and answer key accuracy.

## 45. What if I have a question about the exam paper?
Raise your hand and wait for invigilator assistance. Do not ask questions that could disturb other candidates.

## 46. Can I use multiple languages in my answers?
Answers must be in the official language of instruction. Minor translations may be allowed for clarification but are not encouraged.

## 47. What happens if I accidentally write on the exam paper?
Inform invigilators immediately. They will note the incident, but no marks can be awarded for unintended content.

## 48. How do I handle technical issues with my answer sheet?
Report to invigilators immediately. Replacement sheets may be provided, but time constraints may apply.

## 49. What if I need to reschedule an exam due to personal reasons?
Submit a formal request with valid documentation to the examination committee at least two weeks in advance.

## 50. Can I use notes during the exam?
No. All materials must be submitted before starting. Unauthorized notes may lead to malpractice charges.
