# Examination Rules

## Overview
This document outlines the rules and regulations students must adhere to before, during, and after examinations at [University Name]. These guidelines are designed to ensure a fair, secure, and conducive environment for all candidates. All students are expected to familiarize themselves with these rules and follow them strictly.

---

## Before the Examination

### Eligibility
Students must be officially registered for the examination and have completed all prerequisite coursework or assessments. Only those who meet the eligibility criteria will be permitted to sit for the exam.

### Attendance Requirements
Students must attend mandatory pre-examination orientation sessions held by the academic department. Absence without valid documentation may result in disqualification from the examination.

### Admit Card Requirements
All students must obtain and carry a valid admit card to the examination hall. The admit card must include:
- Full name
- Registration number
- Examination date and time
- Hall/seat number
- Photo of the student
- Signature

Students are required to present their admit card along with a government-issued photo ID (e.g., passport, driving license) for verification.

### Student Identification
Unauthorized entry into the examination hall will be denied. Students must ensure their ID is clearly visible at all times.

### Reporting Time
Students must arrive at the designated reporting time, which is **15 minutes before the commencement of the exam**. Late arrivals will not be permitted to enter after the first 10 minutes of the examination.

### Late Entry Rules
Students arriving late may only enter if:
- They provide a valid reason (e.g., medical emergency) supported by documentary evidence.
- The invigilator approves their entry.
- They are allowed to sit for the exam, but will not be eligible for any additional time beyond the allocated duration.

### Seating Arrangements
Seating is assigned randomly by the examination committee. Students must take their designated seats without requesting changes unless authorized by an invigilator. Sitting in unauthorized seats may lead to disqualification.

### Permitted Materials
Students are allowed to bring:
- Pens, pencils, and erasers.
- A basic scientific calculator (non-programmable).
- A transparent water bottle (no labels).
- A single sheet of handwritten notes (maximum 8.5 x 11 inches).

### Prohibited Materials
The following items are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disqualification:
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, or any electronic devices.
- Communication gadgets (e.g., Bluetooth earpieces).
- Printed materials, textbooks, or study guides.
- Any form of recording equipment.
- Unauthorized calculators (e.g., financial or graphing calculators).

### Calculator Policy
Only basic scientific calculators are permitted. Calculators must be non-programmable and have a clear display. Students may not use calculators for purposes other than mathematical computations.

### Mobile Phone Policy
Mobile phones must be switched off and stored in a sealed container provided by the examination center. Possession of a mobile phone during the exam will result in immediate disqualification, regardless of intent.

### Smartwatch Policy
Smartwatches are prohibited unless explicitly authorized by the examination committee. Wearing a smartwatch during the exam will lead to disqualification.

---

## During the Examination

### Allowed Actions
- Use permitted materials (e.g., pens, calculators).
- Write notes on the provided rough work sheet.
- Seek clarification on ambiguous questions from invigilators.
- Take short bathroom breaks if required.

### Prohibited Actions
- Communicating with other students in any form (verbal, written, or electronic).
- Using unauthorized materials (e.g., textbooks, cheat sheets).
- Attempting to access or use prohibited devices.
- Leaving the examination hall without permission.
- Writing on or altering the admit card.

### Behavior Expectations
Students must maintain a quiet and respectful demeanor. Disruptive behavior, such as loud talking, shouting, or using profanity, will result in disciplinary action.

### Bathroom Breaks
Students may leave the examination hall for bathroom breaks only with prior approval from an invigilator. They must re-enter within 10 minutes of their absence. Prolonged absence may lead to disqualification.

### Rough Work
Rough work must be done on the designated rough work sheet provided. Students are not allowed to write on exam question papers or use personal notebooks for calculations.

### Answer Booklet Rules
- All answers must be written in the designated answer booklet.
- Use only black or blue ink for writing.
- Avoid using correction fluid or tape; instead, strike through errors and initial them.
- Ensure all pages are properly numbered and stapled at the end.

### Extra Sheets
Students may request additional sheets for rough work if needed. Extra sheets must be signed by an invigilator and attached to the answer booklet.

---

## After the Examination

### Submission Process
Students must submit their answer booklets to the invigilator before leaving the examination hall. Late submissions will not be accepted, and students may face disciplinary action.

### Leaving the Examination Hall
Students are required to leave the examination hall immediately after submitting their answer booklet. Remaining in the hall without permission is a violation of exam rules.

### Reporting Issues
If students encounter any issues (e.g., damaged materials, technical malfunctions), they must report them to an invigilator immediately. Failure to report such issues may result in disqualification.

---

## Common Mistakes

### Examples of Acceptable Behavior
- Using a basic scientific calculator for mathematical computations.
- Writing notes on the rough work sheet during the exam.
- Asking clarifying questions about ambiguous instructions from invigilators.
- Taking a short bathroom break with prior approval.

### Examples of Prohibited Behavior
- Sharing answers with other students.
- Using a mobile phone or smartwatch during the exam.
- Bringing unauthorized materials (e.g., textbooks, cheat sheets).
- Writing on the examination question paper.
- Leaving the hall without permission after submitting the answer booklet.

### Common Student Mistakes
1. **Not bringing the admit card**: Students often forget to carry their admit card, leading to denied entry.
2. **Using prohibited devices**: Many students attempt to use mobile phones or smartwatches during exams, resulting in disqualification.
3. **Writing on question papers**: Students sometimes write on the exam paper instead of using the answer booklet, which is strictly prohibited.
4. **Arriving late**: Late arrivals are often not allowed to enter after the first 10 minutes of the exam.
5. **Ignoring behavior rules**: Disruptive behavior can lead to immediate ejection from the examination hall.

---

## Student Responsibilities
Students are responsible for:
- Adhering to all examination rules and regulations.
- Ensuring they have all required materials (admit card, ID) before the exam.
- Reporting any issues or discrepancies immediately.
- Maintaining academic integrity by not cheating or assisting others in cheating.
- Respecting invigilators and other students during the examination process.

Failure to comply with these responsibilities may result in disciplinary action, including disqualification from the examination and potential academic sanctions.
