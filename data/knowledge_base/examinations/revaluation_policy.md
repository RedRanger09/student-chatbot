# Revaluation Policy

## Overview

Revaluation is a formal process allowing students to request a review of their examination results to ensure accuracy and fairness. This policy outlines the procedures, eligibility criteria, and guidelines for submitting revaluation requests. It distinguishes between **rechecking** (a routine verification of marks) and **revaluation** (a comprehensive reassessment of exam papers). Students must adhere to deadlines, provide required documentation, and understand the potential outcomes of their request.

### Eligibility
Students who have completed an examination cycle are eligible to apply for revaluation. This includes:
- All undergraduate and postgraduate students
- Candidates who believe there may be errors in their results
- Those with valid academic grievances (e.g., missing answer sheets, discrepancies in marks)

### Application Procedure
1. **Submission**: Apply through the university’s online portal or designated office within the specified deadline.
2. **Documentation**: Provide original exam papers, answer scripts, and any supporting evidence of errors.
3. **Fee Payment**: Pay the revaluation fee as per institutional guidelines.

### Deadlines
- **Last Date for Submission**: 15 working days from the announcement of results.
- **Processing Time**: 20–30 days post-submission (varies by department).

### Required Documents
- Original exam paper and answer script
- Proof of enrollment (ID card or student portal login)
- Written explanation of discrepancies (if applicable)

### Revaluation Fee
- **Fee Structure**: ₹500 per subject for undergraduate courses; ₹1,000 per subject for postgraduate programs.
- **Refund Policy**: Fees are non-refundable unless the revaluation request is rejected.

---

## Eligibility

Students must meet specific criteria to qualify for revaluation:
- **Academic Standing**: Only students who have completed all assigned coursework and exams are eligible.
- **Timely Submission**: Requests must be submitted within the specified deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted.
- **Valid Grounds**: Revaluation is only permitted if there is a plausible cause for error (e.g., missing questions, incorrect grading).

**Note**: Students with incomplete coursework or unresolved academic issues are ineligible until these matters are resolved.

---

## Procedure

### Step 1: Submit Request
Access the university’s revaluation portal via [https://university.edu/revaluation](https://university.edu/revaluation) or visit the registrar’s office. Fill out the application form, specifying:
- Subject(s) for revaluation
- Nature of discrepancy (e.g., missing answer, grading error)
- Supporting documents

### Step 2: Pay Fee
Make payment via the online portal using a valid credit card, net banking, or UPI. Retain the receipt as proof of payment.

### Step 3: Wait for Review
The revaluation team will review your request and conduct a thorough check of the answer sheets. This process may involve:
- Reassessing grading criteria
- Verifying calculations
- Cross-referencing with original scripts

### Step 4: Receive Outcome
Results will be communicated via email or the portal within 30 days. You will receive:
- Updated marksheet (if changes are made)
- Explanation of findings (if no changes are approved)

---

## Outcomes

There are three possible outcomes after revaluation:
1. **Grade Increase**: If errors are found, your grade will be adjusted upward.
2. **Grade Decrease**: If the review reveals incorrect grading, your grade may decrease.
3. **No Change**: If the results are confirmed accurate, no adjustments will be made.

**Updated Marksheets**: All changes will be reflected in the official records and sent to the respective departments.

---

## Limitations

- **Finality of Results**: Revaluation is not a guarantee of grade improvement. The decision is final once communicated.
- **No Multiple Requests**: Students cannot submit multiple revaluation requests for the same subject within 6 months.
- **No Cash Refunds**: Fees are non-refundable, even if the request is rejected.

**Important Note**: Revaluation does not replace academic misconduct investigations. Students found to have cheated or falsified documents will face disciplinary action regardless of revaluation results.

## Frequently Asked Situations

### Appropriate Cases for Revaluation
- Missing questions on the answer sheet
- Incorrect grading of a section (e.g., partial credit awarded incorrectly)
- Discrepancy between marked and unmarked scripts
- Evidence of administrative errors (e.g., wrong subject code assigned)

### Inappropriate Cases
- Requesting revaluation due to poor preparation or low performance
- Seeking changes to subjective answers where no error is evident
- Submitting a request after the deadline
- Failing to provide required documentation

**Common Misconceptions**
- **Myth**: Revaluation guarantees a grade increase.  
  **Reality**: It only corrects errors; it does not improve performance.
- **Myth**: Rechecking and revaluation are the same process.  
  **Reality**: Rechecking is a routine verification, while revaluation involves a full reassessment of all answers.
