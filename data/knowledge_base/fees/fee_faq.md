# University Fees FAQ

## Tuition Fees
### What are the tuition fee structures for different programs?
Tuition fees vary significantly based on program type, faculty, and specializations. Undergraduate engineering programs typically cost $12,000 annually, while humanities programs range from $8,500 to $10,000. Postgraduate courses may charge between $15,000-$25,000 per year depending on research intensity and lab requirements. Specialized programs like medicine or law have separate fee schedules with additional clinical or fieldwork costs.

### How do I calculate my tuition fees for multiple semesters?
The university uses a semester-based calculation model where each term's fee is 60% of the annual rate. For example, if your annual tuition is $12,000, you'll pay $7,200 per semester. Part-time students receive prorated rates based on credit hours completed, with adjustments made quarterly for changing enrollment statuses.

### What happens if I can't afford my tuition fees?
Students facing financial difficulties should contact the Student Support Office immediately. They offer fee payment plans, emergency scholarships, and deferment options. Documentation like income statements or family hardship proofs are required for any waiver applications. Approved students may qualify for reduced interest rates on installment plans.

### Can I get a refund if I withdraw from a course?
Refund policies depend on withdrawal timing. Full refunds (75%) are available within the first two weeks of the semester, decreasing to 10% after six weeks. No refunds are issued after the add/drop period. Students who withdraw entirely may receive pro-rata refunds based on their academic standing and withdrawal date.

### Are there any additional costs for laboratory equipment?
Yes, specialized programs like engineering or sciences charge a $500 annual lab fee covering materials and equipment usage. This is separate from tuition and must be paid alongside regular fees. Students should check their program-specific fee breakdown in the admission package.

## Hostel Fees
### What are the different hostel accommodation options available?
The university offers three main categories: Standard Dormitory (shared rooms, $600/month), Premium Ensuite (private bathrooms, $850/month), and Family Housing (for extended stays, $1,200/month). Each category includes utilities, maintenance, and access to campus facilities. Optional services like internet or laundry incur extra charges.

### Can I request a room change mid-semester?
Room changes are allowed only under exceptional circumstances like medical emergencies or academic disruptions. Students must submit formal requests with supporting documentation (medical certificates, police reports) to the hostel administration. Approval is granted on a case-by-case basis and may involve relocating to another available room.

### What happens if I need to extend my hostel stay beyond graduation?
Students requiring extended stays must apply at least one month before their intended departure date. The hostel will assess availability and academic necessity while reviewing financial capacity. Additional fees apply for the extension period, calculated pro-rata based on months requested.

### Are there any special provisions for students with disabilities?
Yes, the university provides accessible housing options with wheelchair-friendly facilities, sensory rooms, and 24/7 support services. Students should contact the disability office at least three months before enrollment to arrange accommodations. These are included in the standard hostel fee but require prior approval.

## Installments
### How do I apply for an installment payment plan?
Students can request installments through the ERP portal during registration. The system automatically generates a 3-6 month payment schedule based on tuition amounts and financial aid status. Applications must be submitted before the first semester's due date to avoid late fees.

### What if I miss an installment payment deadline?
Missed payments incur a 1.5% monthly penalty fee on overdue balances. Students must pay within 10 days of the due date to avoid enrollment restrictions or academic probation. Late fees are waived for unavoidable circumstances like natural disasters with proper documentation.

### Can I adjust my installment plan if my financial situation changes?
Yes, students can request payment plan modifications by submitting updated financial information through the finance office. Approved adjustments may include extended terms, reduced monthly payments, or temporary suspension of installments for up to six months.

## Refunds
### What is the refund policy for hostel deposits?
Hostel deposits are non-refundable unless students vacate before the academic year ends. A 50% refund is available if a student leaves within three months of enrollment, with additional conditions for medical or family emergencies. All refund requests must be submitted through the finance office with supporting documentation.

### Can I get a partial refund if I withdraw mid-semester?
Partial refunds are only available if you withdraw before the final drop deadline. The percentage depends on remaining days and withdrawal type (voluntary/involuntary). For example, withdrawing 10 days before semester end would entitle you to approximately 40% of tuition fees.

### What happens if I overpay my fees?
Overpayments are credited toward future semesters or refunded upon request. Students should contact the finance office to process refunds within one academic year of payment. Credits may also be applied to outstanding balances or used for course withdrawals.

## Late Payment
### What is considered a late fee for tuition payments?
Late fees apply if payments are received after the scheduled due date. The penalty is 1.5% per month on overdue amounts, with additional charges for missed installments. Students must pay within 10 days of the due date to avoid academic probation or enrollment restrictions.

### Can I request an extension for late payment?
Extensions are granted only for unavoidable circumstances like medical emergencies or natural disasters. Students must submit formal requests with supporting documentation through the finance office. Approved extensions may delay penalties but require immediate payment upon resolution.

## Scholarships
### How do I apply for merit-based scholarships?
Merit scholarships are awarded based on academic performance in entrance exams and previous qualifications. Applications open annually in February, requiring transcripts, recommendation letters, and a personal statement. Shortlisted candidates undergo interviews in March with the scholarship committee.

### Are there need-based financial aid options?
Yes, the university offers need-based scholarships for students from low-income backgrounds. Applicants must submit income statements, bank records, and proof of family circumstances through the Student Support Office. Awards are determined based on both academic merit and financial need.

## Failed Transactions
### What should I do if my payment fails?
Failed transactions require immediate action to avoid late fees. Students should contact the finance office within 5 business days to resolve issues like incorrect bank details or insufficient funds. Repeat failures may result in enrollment restrictions until payments are cleared.

### How long does it take to process failed transaction corrections?
Most failed transactions are resolved within 3 business days if corrected promptly. Students must resubmit payment information through the ERP portal with updated banking details. Failed attempts over 5 days may incur additional fees for processing delays.

## Payment Receipts
### Where can I download my fee receipt?
Receipts are available in the student portal under "My Payments" section. They include detailed breakdowns of tuition, hostel, and miscellaneous charges along with transaction dates and reference numbers. Hard copies can be printed from the finance office upon request.

## ERP
### How do I access my ERP account?
Students receive login credentials via email after admission. The ERP system allows viewing fee balances, payment history, and academic records. Regular updates are sent to the student portal for any changes in financial obligations or course enrollments.

## Bank Transfer
### What are the bank transfer details for tuition payments?
Bank transfers should be made to the university's designated account at [bank name], with transaction references including student ID and fee type. Payments must be received by the due date to avoid late fees, with proof of payment uploaded through the ERP portal within 5 days.

## International Students
### Are there special fee structures for international students?
Yes, international students pay a higher tuition rate covering additional costs like visa processing, language support, and cultural integration programs. They also incur a $200 per semester administrative fee for immigration documentation and health insurance coverage.

## Security Deposits
### What is the security deposit amount for hostel stays?
A non-refundable security deposit of $500 is required upon admission to cover potential damages or unpaid balances. This is deducted from the final refund calculation if students vacate before the academic year ends. Deposits are returned in full under normal circumstances.

## Miscellaneous Charges
### What are the common miscellaneous fees?
Miscellaneous charges include library fines ($1/day), exam reprints ($5 per page), and lost ID card replacements ($20). These are separate from tuition and hostel fees but must be paid alongside regular payments to avoid academic restrictions.
