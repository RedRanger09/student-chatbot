## Grace Period
A grace period of **7 calendar days** is provided after the payment deadline to rectify late submissions. During this period, you may submit your payment without incurring full penalties. However, partial fees may still apply depending on the nature of the transaction.

### Eligibility for Grace Period
- Students who have submitted a valid payment request form before the deadline.
- Payments made within 7 days of the original due date.
- No prior outstanding balances or previous late fee violations.

## Late Fee Calculation
Late fees are calculated based on the type of payment and the duration of delinquency. The following formulas apply:

### Simple Interest Formula
```
Late Fee = Principal Amount × (1 + Rate × Time)
```
Where:
- **Principal Amount** is the original amount owed.
- **Rate** is the annual interest rate (typically 5% for late fees).
- **Time** is the number of days past the due date.

### Example Calculation
If a student owes $1,000 and submits payment 10 days after the deadline:
```
Late Fee = $1,000 × (1 + 0.05 × 10/365) ≈ $1,013.70
```

## Academic Consequences
Failure to meet payment deadlines may result in the following academic restrictions:

### Registration Hold
- **Impact:** Prevents enrollment in new courses or registration for subsequent semesters.
- **Duration:** Until all outstanding fees are settled.

### Exam Restriction
- **Impact:** Prohibits participation in final exams and assessment activities.
- **Duration:** Until payment is received.

### Result Withholding
- **Impact:** Delays the release of academic results and transcripts.
- **Duration:** Until payment is received.

### ERP Restrictions
- **Impact:** Limits access to essential resources such as library services, online learning platforms, and campus facilities.
- **Duration:** Until outstanding balances are cleared.

## Penalties
In addition to late fees, the following penalties may apply for repeated or severe non-payment:

### Financial Sanctions
- **Action:** Suspension of financial aid eligibility.
- **Impact:** Loss of scholarships, grants, or loans.

### Academic Dismissal
- **Action:** Mandatory withdrawal from all courses.
- **Impact:** Requires reapplication for admission in the next academic cycle.

### Legal Action
- **Action:** Pursuit of legal remedies to recover outstanding debts.
- **Impact:** Potential fines or court involvement.

## Waiver Requests
Students may request a waiver for late fees under exceptional circumstances. To qualify, you must submit:

1. A written explanation of the situation (e.g., medical emergency, financial hardship).
2. Supporting documentation (e.g., hospital records, income proof).
3. A completed waiver form available on the university portal.

### Approval Process
- Submissions are reviewed by the Financial Aid Office within **10 business days**.
- Waivers are granted only for verifiable emergencies and may be subject to conditions or partial approval.

## Appeals
If you believe a late fee was applied incorrectly, you may appeal the decision through the following steps:

1. Submit an appeal form via the university portal.
2. Provide evidence of payment or circumstances affecting your ability to pay on time.
3. Await review by the Financial Services Committee within **15 business days**.

### Appeal Outcomes
- **Approved:** Fee waived or adjusted.
- **Denied:** Fee remains in effect; you may request a reconsideration within 7 days.

## Common Student Mistakes
Many students face late fees due to preventable errors. Here are the most common mistakes:

1. **Missing Payment Deadlines:** Failing to check portal notifications or calendar reminders.
2. **Incorrect Payment Methods:** Using expired cards or incorrect account numbers.
3. **Overlooking Fine Print:** Not reading terms and conditions for additional fees.
4. **Neglecting Grace Periods:** Submitting payments after the 7-day grace period has expired.
5. **Ignoring Multiple Deadlines:** Confusing course enrollment dates with tuition deadlines.
