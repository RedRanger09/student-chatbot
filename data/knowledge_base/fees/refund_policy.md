# University Refund Policy

## Overview
This policy outlines the refund procedures for tuition fees, hostel charges, security deposits, and other institutional payments. Refunds are processed according to established guidelines that balance institutional financial obligations with student needs. All refunds must be requested through the official portal within specified timelines.

## Refund Eligibility
Refunds are generally available for:
- Tuition fees paid in full or partially
- Hostel accommodation charges
- Security deposits
- Examination and service fees

Eligibility depends on:
1. Withdrawal date relative to semester start
2. Completion of registration formalities
3. Absence of outstanding obligations
4. Compliance with institutional regulations

## Refund Categories

### Admission Withdrawal
Full refund (85%) available if withdrawal occurs:
- Before 7 days of academic session commencement
- With valid medical/force majeure documentation
- For international students, within 14 days of arrival

### Semester Withdrawal
Refund percentages based on withdrawal timing:
- First 30 days: 90% tuition fee
- Days 31-60: 75% tuition fee
- Days 61-90: 50% tuition fee
- After 90 days: No refund

### Hostel Refund
Pro-rated based on occupancy period:
- Full refund for cancellations before 30 days of admission
- 75% refund for cancellations 31-60 days prior
- 50% refund for cancellations 61-90 days prior
- No refund after 90 days

### Security Deposit Refund
Refundable upon:
- Completion of academic session
- Submission of final academic records
- Absence of outstanding fees
- Validity of residence certificate

### Examination Refund
Available for:
- Unsuccessful exam attempts (with valid documentation)
- Duplicate payment corrections
- Registration errors within 7 days of payment

## Refund Workflow
1. **Request Submission** - Submit refund request through the portal with required documents
2. **Document Verification** - Administrative team reviews supporting evidence
3. **Approval Process** - Department head approves eligible claims
4. **Payment Processing** - Finance department initiates refund
5. **Bank Disbursement** - Refund processed within 10 business days
6. **Receipt Issuance** - Official receipt generated for record-keeping

## Required Documents
Basic documentation includes:
- Admission confirmation
- Payment receipts
- Withdrawal/transfer letter
- Medical certificates (for emergencies)
- Academic records

Specific to category:
- For hostel refunds: Lease agreement copy
- For examination refunds: Exam result slips
- For duplicate payments: Bank transaction statements

## Processing Timeline
| Stage | Duration | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Document Verification | 5 business days | May extend for complex cases |
| Approval Process | 3 business days | Department head review |
| Payment Processing | 2 business days | Banking system constraints |
| Disbursement | 10 business days | Post-approval processing |

## Refund Calculation
Formula:  
`Refund Amount = (Paid Amount × Eligibility Percentage) - Non-refundable Charges`

Example calculation:
For a $12,000 tuition payment with 75% eligibility:
$12,000 × 0.75 = $9,000 refund

## Non-refundable Charges
These fees are not eligible for refund:
- Application processing fees
- Document verification charges
- Late registration penalties
- Examination misconduct fines
- Equipment damage deposits
- Optional service charges

## Special Cases

### Medical Emergencies
- Valid hospital certificate required
- Doctor's note within 7 days of withdrawal
- Approval from Student Welfare Committee
- Refund calculated based on remaining academic period

### Duplicate Payment
- Proof of duplicate transaction required
- Bank statement showing double payment
- Submission within 15 days of discovery
- Refund processed as full reimbursement

### Admission Cancellation
- Official cancellation notice from admissions
- Valid reason documented in writing
- Refund calculated based on admission fees paid
- No refund for cancelled admissions after session start

### Scholarship Adjustments
- Automatic recalculations for:
  - Academic performance changes
  - Course load adjustments
  - Program transfers
- Documentation required within 30 days of change
- Refunds processed according to revised eligibility

## Common Mistakes
1. Submitting incomplete documentation
2. Missing the refund deadline (portal closes 30 days post-withdrawal)
3. Not providing required medical certificates
4. Failing to update academic records
5. Submitting duplicate payment claims without proof
6. Not notifying about course load changes
7. Missing scholarship adjustment deadlines

## Appeals Process
1. **Form Submission** - File appeal within 15 days of rejection
2. **Evidence Submission** - Provide additional documentation
3. **Panel Review** - Academic committee evaluates case
4. **Decision Notification** - Final decision within 20 business days
5. **Reconsideration** - One-time request for further review
