# New Student Onboarding Guide

## Before Arrival

### Admission Confirmation
1. **Verify your admission letter** has been received and contains all necessary details (program, start date, campus location).
2. **Confirm your enrollment status** via the university's ERP system to ensure your seat is reserved.

### Fee Payment
3. **Pay the tuition fee** by the specified deadline to avoid cancellation of your admission. 
   - Use the online payment portal accessible through your ERP account.
   - Retain payment receipts for future reference.

### Document Verification
4. **Submit original documents** (passport copy, academic transcripts, recommendation letters) via the university's portal.
5. **Ensure all documents are legible and properly formatted** as per the university's guidelines to avoid delays.

### Student ID Card
6. **Prepare a recent passport-sized photo** (size: 3.5 x 4.5 cm, white background) for your student ID card.
7. **Check the ID card issuance schedule** on the university website for expected timeline.

### ERP Activation
8. **Activate your ERP account** using the credentials provided in your admission letter.
9. **Familiarize yourself with ERP features** (course registration, grade tracking, financial aid portal).

### Email Activation
10. **Create your university email account** using the format: `studentID@university.edu`.
11. **Update your email password regularly** and enable two-factor authentication for security.

### Timetable Access
12. **Log in to the ERP system** to view your tentative timetable.
13. **Note down important dates** (exams, assignments, project deadlines).

### Course Registration
14. **Review course prerequisites** to ensure you're eligible for all selected courses.
15. **Register for courses** during the designated registration period to avoid last-minute issues.

### Hostel Reporting
16. **Confirm your hostel allocation** via ERP and note down your room number.
17. **Prepare bedding materials** (bedsheet, pillow, blanket) as per hostel guidelines.

### Transport Registration
18. **Register for campus transport** if applicable by submitting your vehicle details through the portal.
19. **Check transport schedules** to plan your commute effectively.

### Library Membership
20. **Activate your library account** using your student ID to access digital resources and books.
21. **Familiarize yourself with library rules** (e.g., borrowing limits, late return penalties).

### Wi-Fi Registration
22. **Register for campus Wi-Fi** using your student credentials to ensure internet connectivity.
23. **Note down the Wi-Fi password** and security settings for future reference.

### Orientation Program
24. **Mark your calendar for orientation week** – a crucial event to meet faculty, peers, and understand campus facilities.
25. **Prepare questions about academic policies, campus life, and support services** to ask during orientation.

### Academic Advisor Meeting
26. **Schedule a meeting with your academic advisor** to discuss course selection, career planning, and academic resources.
27. **Bring any relevant documents** (transcripts, career goals) to make the most of your advising session.

### Campus Tour
28. **Plan to attend the campus tour** to explore facilities like labs, libraries, sports complexes, and student centers.
29. **Note important locations** (emergency exits, medical center, IT help desk).

### Health Documentation
30. **Submit health certificates** (e.g., vaccination records, medical insurance proof) as required by the university.
31. **Register with the campus health center** to access medical services during your stay.

### Scholarship Verification
32. **Verify scholarship eligibility** and ensure all required documents are submitted on time.
33. **Keep track of scholarship deadlines** to avoid disqualification.

### Student Clubs
34. **Explore student clubs and societies** listed on the university website to find interests that match your hobbies.
35. **Attend club meetings during orientation** to connect with like-minded peers.

## First Week

### Weekly Goals
- **Day 1**: Set up ERP account, attend orientation session, meet academic advisor
- **Day 2**: Register for courses, activate library membership, complete hostel check-in
- **Day 3**: Attend campus tour, join student clubs, submit health documentation
- **Day 4**: Finalize course schedule, configure Wi-Fi, register for transport
- **Day 5**: Review timetable, attend club meetings, prepare for first exams

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Missing document submission** – Ensure all required forms are submitted on time.
- **Late fee payment** – Pay tuition fees by the deadline to avoid academic penalties.
- **Ignoring orientation sessions** – Orientation provides critical information about campus life and resources.
- **Poor time management** – Use the timetable effectively to balance academics and personal activities.

## First Month

### Key Activities
- **Week 1**: Focus on course registration, hostel setup, and familiarizing with campus facilities.
- **Week 2**: Engage in club activities, attend academic workshops, and start coursework.
- **Week 3**: Mid-term preparations, seek academic support if needed, and adjust to the campus lifestyle.
- **Week 4**: Finalize project timelines, participate in cultural events, and network with peers.

### Preparation Tips
- **Stay organized** by using digital tools for scheduling and document management.
- **Attend all orientation sessions** to maximize your first-week experience.
- **Build a support network** by joining clubs and participating in campus activities.
- **Maintain good health** by utilizing the campus medical center and following dietary guidelines.

## Helpful Tips
- **Use the ERP system regularly** for updates on course schedules, grades, and financial aid.
- **Explore campus facilities** during free time to make the most of your university experience.
- **Seek academic help promptly** if you're struggling with coursework or need guidance.
- **Stay connected with peers** through social media groups and club meetings to build a strong community.
