# Hostel Facilities

The hostel facilities at [University Name] are designed to provide a comfortable and supportive environment for students. - **CCTV**: CCTV cameras for safety and monitoring.
- **Parking**: Secure parking spaces for residents and visitors.
- **Bicycle Parking**: Designated areas for cyclists.
- **Guest Rooms**: Temporary accommodations for visiting family members.
- **Lift Facilities**: Elevators for easy access to upper floors.
- **Accessibility Features**: Ramps, elevators, and designated accessible restrooms.
- **Maintenance Requests**: A streamlined process for reporting repairs and service needs.

## Study Facilities
The hostel prioritizes academic success by providing dedicated study spaces. The **reading room** is a quiet zone ideal for focused work, while **study halls** offer collaborative areas with ample seating. These facilities are equipped with adequate lighting, power outlets, and internet access to support both individual and group study sessions.

- **Reading Room**: Located on the ground floor, this area features bookshelves, desks, and ergonomic chairs. It is open during regular library hours.
- **Study Halls**: Available in each block, these halls are designed for group discussions and project work. They include whiteboards and access to printers.
- **Library Access**: Residents can borrow books from the university library using their hostel ID cards.

---

## Recreation
To promote a healthy lifestyle, the hostel offers various recreational facilities:

- **Recreation Areas**: Open-air spaces with seating and games for relaxation.
- **Indoor Games**: The gymnasium includes table tennis tables, carrom boards, and indoor cricket facilities.
- **Gym**: A state-of-the-art fitness center with cardio machines, weights, and yoga areas. Gym access is available to all residents during specified hours.

---

## Utilities
The hostel ensures seamless utility services for daily living:

- **Water Supply**: Clean water is provided through a centralized system. Residents can access drinking water from designated taps.
- **Electricity**: The hostel has a dedicated electrical substation with backup generators to ensure uninterrupted power supply.
- **Laundry**: On-site washing machines and dryers are available in the laundry room, which operates on a first-come, first-served basis.
- **Housekeeping**: Regular cleaning of common areas, including bathrooms and lounges, is conducted by trained staff.

---

## Security
Safety is a top priority at the hostel:

- **CCTV Surveillance**: High-definition cameras are installed in key areas for 24/7 monitoring.
- **Security Personnel**: Trained guards patrol the premises to ensure the safety of residents.
- **Access Control**: Only registered residents can enter restricted areas, with biometric or card-based entry systems.

---

## Maintenance
Residents are encouraged to report any issues promptly:

- **Maintenance Requests**: Use the online portal or contact the hostel office to submit service requests. Repairs are typically addressed within 24 hours.
- **Common Problems**: Issues such as leaking taps, faulty lights, or broken furniture can be reported through the maintenance system.

---

## Frequently Used Services
Key services that residents frequently use include:

- **Medical Room**: Located near the hostel office, this area is staffed by trained personnel for minor injuries and illnesses. Emergency care is provided in collaboration with the university's medical center.
- **Parking**: Designated parking spaces are available for residents and guests. Parking permits are issued to registered students.
- **Guest Rooms**: Temporary accommodations are available for family visits, subject to availability.
- **Lift Facilities**: Elevators are maintained regularly to ensure safe and efficient access to all floors.
- **Accessibility Features**: The hostel is fully accessible to individuals with disabilities, including ramps, elevators, and designated restrooms.

---

## Eligibility
All registered students residing in the hostel are eligible for these facilities. International students and visiting scholars may also apply for temporary accommodations based on university guidelines.

---

## Usage Rules
- **Wi-Fi**: Internet access is restricted to hostel residents only.
- **Laundry**: Use of laundry machines requires a valid hostel ID card.
- **Gym**: Residents must wear appropriate attire and follow safety guidelines.
- **Medical Room**: Unauthorized individuals may not use the medical facilities without permission.

---

## Availability
Most facilities are available 24/7, except for maintenance periods. The reading room and study halls operate during library hours. Gym access is available from 6 AM to 10 PM.

---

## Reporting Facility Issues
Residents should report any facility issues through the online portal or by contacting the hostel office directly. Detailed descriptions of problems will help expedite repairs.

---

## Common Problems
- **Wi-Fi Outages**: Contact the IT support team for assistance.
- **Laundry Machine Malfunction**: Report via the maintenance system.
- **Gym Equipment Issues**: Notify staff immediately.
- **Security Concerns**: Inform the hostel office or security personnel.
