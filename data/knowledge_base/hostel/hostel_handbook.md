# Hostel Handbook

## Overview  
The University Hostel is a residential facility designed to provide a safe, supportive, and academically conducive environment for students. Located on campus, the hostel offers modern amenities, 24/7 security, and essential services to ensure a comfortable living experience. All undergraduate students who meet the eligibility criteria are encouraged to apply for hostel admission. This handbook outlines policies, procedures, and guidelines to help students navigate their stay effectively.

## Hostel Admission  
**Eligibility**:  
- Full-time undergraduate students enrolled in the university  
- Minimum CGPA of 2.5 (on a 4.0 scale)  
- No pending disciplinary actions  

**Application Process**:  
1. Submit an online application via the university portal by April 30th each year.  
2. Attach academic transcripts, a recent passport-sized photo, and proof of income (for non-scholarship applicants).  
3. Attend an interview with the hostel committee to assess suitability.  

**Admission Timeline**:  
- Applications open: January 1st  
- Interview round: March 15th–20th  
- Admission decisions: April 5th  
- Confirmation deadline: April 30th  

## Room Allocation  
**Allocation Criteria**:  
Rooms are assigned based on department, year of study, and availability. Priority is given to students with scholarships or those in their first year.  

**Room Types**:  
- **Standard Sharing**: Shared with one roommate (2 beds, shared bathroom)  
- **Double Occupancy**: Two single beds (private bathroom)  
- **Single Occupancy**: Private room with en-suite facilities (available for scholarship recipients)  

**Preferences**:  
Students may request specific room types or roommate preferences during the application. Requests are considered based on availability and academic standing.

## Hostel Fees  
**Annual Charges**:  
| Fee Type         | Amount (INR) |  
|------------------|--------------|  
| Room Rent        | 150,000      |  
| Mess Charges     | 80,000       |  
| Security Deposit | 30,000       |  
| Utility Charges  | 20,000       |  

**Payment Terms**:  
- First installment due by June 15th  
- Second installment by September 15th  
- Late fees apply for payments after the deadline (5% of total fee)  

## Security Deposit  
The security deposit of INR 30,000 is refundable upon check-out, provided there are no damages beyond normal wear and tear. A deduction may be made for unpaid fees or unauthorized modifications to hostel property.

## Check-In Procedure  
1. Report to the hostel office with original admission documents and a passport-sized photo.  
2. Complete a room inspection form and sign the hostel rules agreement.  
3. Pay the first installment of fees.  
4. Receive keys and access credentials for the room and common areas.  

**Check-In Deadline**: All students must complete check-in by August 10th to avoid late fees.

## Check-Out Procedure  
1. Submit a final room inspection form to the hostel office.  
2. Settle all outstanding dues, including utilities and mess charges.  
3. Return keys and access credentials.  
4. Receive a refundable portion of the security deposit (if applicable).  

**Check-Out Deadline**: Students must vacate by May 15th each year.

## Student Responsibilities  
- Maintain cleanliness in shared spaces and your room.  
- Report maintenance issues to the hostel warden immediately.  
- Adhere to quiet hours (10:30 PM–7:00 AM).  
- Respect fellow residents and follow visitor policy guidelines.  
- Pay fees on time to avoid penalties or eviction.  

## Hostel Administration  
The hostel is managed by a dedicated team of wardens and staff, who oversee daily operations, security, and student welfare. The warden acts as the primary point of contact for all inquiries and complaints.

## Warden Responsibilities  
Wardens are responsible for:  
- Ensuring compliance with hostel rules and regulations  
- Mediating disputes between residents  
- Organizing orientation programs for new students  
- Coordinating maintenance requests and emergency responses  
- Conducting regular inspections of rooms and common areas  

## Resident Conduct  
Violations of hostel rules may result in warnings, fines, or eviction. Examples include:  
- Unauthorized use of electrical appliances (e.g., heaters, toasters)  
- Noise disturbances during quiet hours  
- Possession of prohibited items (e.g., weapons, illegal substances)  
- Failure to pay fees without valid documentation  

## Visitor Policy  
**Allowed Visitors**:  
- Family members and friends are permitted to visit between 10:00 AM–8:00 PM.  
- Students must host visitors in their rooms or common areas.  
- Visitors under 18 require parental consent.  

**Visitor Limits**:  
- Maximum of two guests per room at any time.  
- Visitors staying overnight must register with the hostel office.  

## Quiet Hours  
Quiet hours are enforced from 10:30 PM to 7:00 AM to ensure a peaceful environment for studying and sleeping. Use of loud devices (e.g., speakers, gaming consoles) is prohibited during this period.

## Common Areas  
The hostel features shared spaces such as:  
- **Lounge**: For socializing and study groups  
- **Library**: Access to academic resources and computers  
- **Cafe**: 24/7 food service with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options  
- **Gymnasium**: Fitness equipment and exercise classes  

## Room Maintenance  
Report maintenance issues via the hostel portal or by contacting the warden. Common requests include:  
- Repairing broken lights or plumbing  
- Replacing faulty electrical outlets  
- Cleaning shared bathrooms and kitchens  

**Response Time**: Minor repairs are addressed within 24 hours; major issues (e.g., leaks, structural damage) are prioritized within 12 hours.

## Complaints  
Students can file complaints through the hostel portal or in person with the warden. Complaints must include:  
- A clear description of the issue  
- Date and time of occurrence  
- Supporting evidence (if applicable)  

**Resolution Timeline**: Most complaints are resolved within 5 working days. Serious issues (e.g., harassment, safety concerns) are escalated to the university administration immediately.

## Emergency Procedures  
In case of emergencies:  
1. Activate the hostel's alarm system or contact the warden immediately.  
2. Evacuate the building via designated emergency exits.  
3. Follow instructions from staff for shelter-in-place or re-entry.  

**Emergency Contacts**:  
- Fire Department: 9988 (campus line)  
- Medical Aid: 108 (campus line)  
- Police: 100 (campus line)  

## Hostel Safety  
The hostel employs round-the-clock security with CCTV surveillance, biometric access control, and regular patrols. Students are encouraged to keep personal belongings secure and report any suspicious activity.

## Fire Safety  
Fire alarms are installed in all dormitory blocks. Students must:  
- Know the location of fire extinguishers and emergency exits  
- Participate in annual fire safety drills  
- Avoid overloading electrical sockets or using unsafe appliances  

## Electricity Usage  
Electricity is metered per room. Excessive usage (e.g., leaving lights on overnight) may result in higher utility charges. Use energy-efficient bulbs and unplug devices when not in use.

## Internet Availability  
High-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hostel. Students can access it via their university credentials. Monthly internet usage limits are enforced to ensure fair distribution.

## Laundry Services  
A coin-operated laundry facility is available in the basement of the hostel building. Students may use it for washing and drying clothes, with a minimum charge of INR 50 per cycle.

## Medical Support  
The hostel has a on-call medical officer available during office hours. For urgent health concerns, students can visit the university's campus clinic or contact the emergency services listed above.

## Common Student Mistakes  
1. **Ignoring Quiet Hours**: Using loud devices late at night leads to warnings and fines.  
2. **Not Reporting Repairs**: Delayed maintenance can worsen issues and affect living conditions.  
3. **Failing to Pay Fees**: Late payments result in loss of room privileges and possible eviction.  
4. **Overloading Electrical Outlets**: This poses fire hazards and may damage appliances.  
5. **Ignoring Visitor Limits**: Hosting more than two guests risks disciplinary action.  

By following this handbook, students can ensure a safe, productive, and enjoyable stay at the University Hostel.
