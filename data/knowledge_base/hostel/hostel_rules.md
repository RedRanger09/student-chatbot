# Hostel Rules

## General Rules

### Entry and Exit Timings
Students must adhere to the following entry and exit timings:
- **Entry:** 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM (Monday to Saturday)
- **Exit:** 12:00 AM - 6:00 AM (Sunday to Saturday)
Exceptions may be granted for academic events or emergencies, requiring prior approval from the hostel warden.

### Curfew
The curfew time is **11:00 PM** on weekdays and **10:30 PM** on weekends. Students must return to their rooms by these times unless authorized for special circumstances.

### Room Cleanliness
- Rooms must be kept clean and orderly at all times.
- Regular trash disposal and maintenance of hygiene standards are mandatory.
- Weekly room inspections will be conducted by hostel staff. Failure to maintain cleanliness may result in disciplinary action.

### Noise Restrictions
- No loud noises, music, or disturbances are allowed between **10:30 PM and 6:00 AM**.
- Use of audio devices must not interfere with others' sleep or study. Earphones are recommended for private listening.

### Visitors
- Visitors are permitted only during designated hours (9:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Monday to Friday).
- A valid ID and prior approval from the hostel warden are required for visitors.
- Unauthorized entry into restricted areas is prohibited.

### Overnight Guests
- Overnight guests must be approved by the hostel warden and registered in advance.
- Guests must leave by **8:00 AM** the following day unless otherwise authorized.
- Hosting unauthorized overnight guests may result in disciplinary action.

### Ragging Prohibition
- All forms of ragging, bullying, or harassment are strictly prohibited.
- Students found engaging in ragging will face immediate disciplinary measures, including suspension from hostel privileges.

### Smoking
- Smoking is forbidden inside all hostel premises and common areas.
- Designated smoking zones are available outside the hostel compound. Use of tobacco products is restricted to these areas only.

### Alcohol
- Consumption of alcohol is prohibited within hostel premises.
- Possession or use of alcoholic beverages may lead to disciplinary action, including expulsion from the hostel.

### Drugs
- The possession, use, or distribution of illegal drugs is strictly forbidden.
- Violations will result in immediate disciplinary measures and potential legal consequences.

### Cooking Inside Rooms
- Cooking is allowed only in designated areas. Electric kettles and microwave ovens are permitted for personal use.
- Open flames, gas stoves, or any cooking equipment not approved by the hostel management are prohibited.

### Electrical Appliances
- Only approved electrical appliances (listed by the hostel administration) may be used.
- High-power devices require prior permission from the hostel warden. Unauthorized use of electrical appliances may lead to disconnection of power supply.

### Internet Usage
- Students must use hostel-provided internet services responsibly.
- Unauthorized Wi-Fi hotspot creation or misuse of network resources is prohibited.
- Violations may result in temporary suspension of internet privileges.

### Laundry Rules
- Washing and drying of personal belongings must be done within designated laundry areas.
- Use of commercial laundries is restricted to approved vendors. Unauthorized washing machines or dryers are not permitted.

## Discipline

Students found violating hostel rules will face disciplinary actions, which may include:
- **Verbal Warning:** For minor infractions.
- **Written Warning:** For repeated or more serious violations.
- **Suspension of Privileges:** Loss of access to certain facilities (e.g., internet, common areas).
- **Detention:** Temporary isolation from hostel premises for severe violations.
- **Expulsion:** In cases of persistent misconduct, illegal activities, or endangering others.

Disciplinary actions will be recorded in the student's academic record and may affect their eligibility for scholarships or other privileges.

## Safety

### Emergency Procedures
- Students must familiarize themselves with emergency exit routes and fire safety protocols.
- Fire alarms and evacuation drills are conducted regularly. Participation is mandatory.

### Personal Safety
- Students are advised to keep personal belongings secure and report any suspicious activity to hostel authorities.
- Use of campus security services is encouraged for assistance or reporting incidents.

### Health Protocols
- Adherence to health and sanitation guidelines is required, especially during outbreaks or emergencies.
- Vaccination records and health declarations may be mandatory for certain events or periods.

## Visitor Policy

### Approval Process
- All visitors must obtain prior approval from the hostel warden through the designated portal.
- Visitors will be registered with their details, including name, contact information, and purpose of visit.

### Visitor Limits
- Maximum number of visitors per day is limited to 2 guests (excluding family members).
- Frequent or unauthorized visitor activity may result in restrictions on future visits.

### Security Measures
- Visitors are subject to security checks upon entry. Unauthorized items or prohibited substances will lead to immediate dismissal.
- Hostel staff reserves the right to deny access to any visitor deemed a threat to hostel safety.

## Violations

### Acceptable Behaviour
- Adherence to all hostel rules and regulations.
- Respect for fellow students, staff, and property.
- Participation in hostel events and community activities.
- Prompt reporting of incidents or concerns to authorities.

### Prohibited Behaviour
- Disregard for curfew times or entry/exit protocols.
- Unauthorized use of electrical appliances or cooking equipment.
- Harassment, bullying, or ragging towards any individual.
- Possession or consumption of prohibited substances (alcohol, drugs).
- Damage to hostel property without authorization.
- Unauthorized entry into restricted areas or dormitory rooms.
- Violation of internet usage policies or creation of unauthorized Wi-Fi hotspots.

### Common Violations
1. **Noise complaints** during late hours.
2. **Unauthorized visitors** exceeding the allowed limit.
3. **Improper waste disposal** leading to unsanitary conditions.
4. **Use of banned electrical appliances** causing safety hazards.
5. **Failure to report incidents** such as theft or vandalism.

## Student Responsibilities

- Follow all hostel rules and regulations diligently.
- Maintain cleanliness and order in shared spaces.
- Respect the rights and privacy of fellow students.
- Participate in hostel activities and contribute to a positive living environment.
- Report any issues, concerns, or violations promptly to the appropriate authorities.
- Cooperate with hostel staff during inspections or emergency procedures.
