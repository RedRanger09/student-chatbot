# Hostel Mess Policy

## Overview
This document outlines the mess policy for the university hostel, ensuring a hygienic, respectful, and efficient dining environment for all residents. The policy covers meal timings, dietary requirements, fees, dining etiquette, and procedures for addressing concerns or complaints. All students are required to adhere to these guidelines to maintain a harmonious communal living space.

## Meal Timings
Students must observe the following meal schedules:

- **Breakfast:** 7:30 AM - 9:00 AM  
  A light meal to start the day, including options like toast, eggs, milk, and cereal. Students are encouraged to arrive early to avoid long queues and ensure timely consumption.

- **Lunch:** 12:30 PM - 1:30 PM  
  A balanced main meal with a variety of vegetarian and non-vegetarian options. The mess committee ensures that at least 50% of the menu is vegetarian, catering to diverse dietary preferences.

- **Dinner:** 6:30 PM - 8:00 PM  
  A hearty dinner to sustain energy for the evening. Special accommodations are made for students with late classes or extracurricular activities, with extended closing times during exam periods.

- **Snacks:** Available at designated snack counters between 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM and 9:30 PM - 10:30 PM.  
  Light options like fruits, nuts, and beverages are available during these times. Students are reminded to consume snacks in moderation to avoid excessive calorie intake.

## Dining Rules
### Weekly Menu
The mess committee curates a weekly menu that rotates to provide diverse culinary experiences. The menu includes:

- **Vegetarian Options:** Lentil curries, vegetable biryani, paneer tikka masala, and mixed veg stir-fry.  
- **Non-Vegetarian Options:** Chicken curry, mutton kebabs, fish curry, and prawn masala.  
- **Breakfast Items:** Poha, idli, dosa, eggs, and oatmeal.  

The menu is posted in the mess hall weekly, with adjustments made based on student feedback. Students are encouraged to participate in monthly menu surveys to influence future selections.

### Vegetarian Options
Vegetarian meals are clearly marked and available daily. Students with dietary restrictions should notify the mess committee at least 3 days in advance to ensure accommodations are made. The kitchen staff prepares separate vegetarian cooking areas to prevent cross-contamination.

### Special Dietary Requests
Students with allergies or specific dietary needs (e.g., gluten-free, halal, kosher) must submit a written request to the mess committee. The committee will work with the kitchen staff to provide suitable alternatives. For example, students following halal diets will receive separate meal preparations during Ramadan and other Islamic festivals.

## Fees
### Mess Fees
The hostel charges a monthly mess fee of ₹1,500, which covers all meals and snacks. Additional charges may apply for special events or guest meals. Students residing in premium hostel blocks may have slightly higher fees due to enhanced amenities.

### Payment Schedule
- **Monthly Payment:** Due on the 5th of each month.  
- **Late Fee:** A 5% penalty is applied for payments made after the due date.  

Payments can be made via online transfer, cash, or card at the hostel office. Students are advised to set up automatic payments to avoid late fees and ensure uninterrupted access to meals.

## Complaints
### Food Complaints
Students who are dissatisfied with the quality or variety of food should report their concerns to the mess committee within 24 hours of the meal. Complaints can be submitted in writing or through the hostel's online portal. The committee prioritizes resolving issues promptly to maintain student satisfaction.

### Complaint Process
1. **Submit a Written Complaint:** Fill out the complaint form available at the hostel office or on the university intranet. Include specific details about the issue, such as the meal type, date, and time of service.
2. **Wait for Review:** The mess committee will review the complaint and respond within 5 working days. A follow-up meeting may be scheduled to discuss the resolution.
3. **Follow-Up:** If the issue is not resolved, students may escalate the matter to the hostel warden. The warden will mediate between the student and the mess committee to find a satisfactory solution.

### Food Wastage
Students are encouraged to take only what they can consume. Excessive food waste will result in a warning and possible deduction from meal allowances. The mess committee conducts weekly audits to monitor waste levels and adjust portion sizes accordingly.

## Student Responsibilities
### Attendance
Students must attend meals as scheduled. Absences without prior notice may result in reduced meal allocations. Students who miss meals due to medical emergencies must provide a doctor's note to the hostel office for approval.

### Guest Meals
Guests are allowed one complimentary meal per visit, provided they notify the mess committee 24 hours in advance. Additional meals require payment at the standard rate. Guests are expected to adhere to the same dining rules as resident students.

### Hygiene
- Wash hands before and after eating.  
- Use designated utensils and avoid sharing food.  
- Keep dining areas clean by disposing of trash properly.  

The hostel conducts regular hygiene inspections to ensure compliance with health standards. Students who repeatedly violate hygiene rules may face disciplinary action.

### Dining Etiquette
- **Quiet Dining:** Maintain a respectful volume during meal times to avoid disturbing others.  
- **Respectful Behavior:** Avoid aggressive behavior or verbal abuse towards staff.  
- **Table Manners:** Use utensils appropriately and do not waste food.  

The mess committee organizes monthly dining etiquette workshops to educate students on proper table manners and cultural sensitivity.

### Food Wastage
Students are encouraged to take only what they can consume. Excessive food waste will result in a warning and possible deduction from meal allowances. The mess committee conducts weekly audits to monitor waste levels and adjust portion sizes accordingly.

## Common Student Mistakes
1. **Ignoring Meal Timings:** Eating outside designated hours disrupts the mess schedule and may lead to reduced meal options.  
2. **Not Reporting Dietary Needs:** Failing to inform the mess committee about dietary restrictions can result in unsuitable meals.  
3. **Disregarding Hygiene Rules:** Poor hygiene practices can spread illness and affect the entire dining environment.  

Students are advised to review the policy regularly to avoid common mistakes and ensure compliance with hostel regulations.

## Examples of Dining Etiquette
- **Proper Utensil Use:** Use cutlery correctly (e.g., fork on the left, knife on the right).  
- **Avoiding Distractions:** Keep phones on silent mode during meals to maintain a peaceful atmosphere.  
- **Respecting Others' Space:** Do not lean over others or take up more than your share of space at the table.

The mess committee provides visual guides and posters in the dining area to reinforce proper etiquette practices.

## Mess Committee
The mess committee is composed of student representatives and staff members who oversee the quality and management of food services. Students can join the committee by applying through the hostel office. The committee meets bi-weekly to discuss improvements and address concerns. Committee members undergo training on conflict resolution and customer service to enhance their effectiveness.

## Holiday Meal Schedules
During holidays, meal timings may be adjusted. The updated schedule will be posted in the mess hall and communicated via email. Students are encouraged to check for updates before traveling or attending events. Special holiday menus are prepared to accommodate traditional dishes and cultural preferences.
