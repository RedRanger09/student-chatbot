# Borrowing Policy

## Eligibility
All enrolled students, faculty members, and staff of [University Name] are eligible to borrow library materials. Guest researchers and visiting scholars may apply for temporary access by submitting a valid ID and proof of affiliation with an academic institution.

## Borrowing Rules
### Borrowing Limits
- Students: Maximum 5 items per day (10 items during exam periods)
- Faculty/Staff: Maximum 10 items per day
- Guest Researchers: Maximum 3 items for the duration of their stay

### Loan Periods
Standard loan period is 28 days for most materials. Special provisions apply:
- Reference materials: 7 days
- Reserve textbooks: 48 hours
- Audiovisual materials: 14 days
- E-books: 30 days (with automatic renewal)
- Theses/dissertations: Permanently accessible through the library's digital archive

### Book Issue Process
1. Verify your library account status
2. Select materials from the circulation desk or automated retrieval kiosks
3. Present valid ID for verification
4. Complete the checkout form with borrowing details
5. Receive a barcode-enabled RFID tag for tracking

## Renewals
Items may be renewed up to 3 times, provided:
- No other user has placed a hold
- The item is not currently overdue
- The renewal does not exceed the maximum loan period for that material type
Renewals can be requested through the online catalog or at the service desk.

## Returns
### Return Process
1. Place items in designated return slots by 11:59 PM daily
2. For fragile materials, use padded envelopes and request special handling
3. Return all borrowed materials before leaving campus during holidays

### Overdue Books
Items returned after the due date will incur late fees:
- First 7 days: $0.25/day
- 8-14 days: $0.50/day
- 15+ days: $1.00/day
Overdue items may be recalled by other users after 3 consecutive days of overdue.

## Penalties
### Lost Books
- Replacement fee: Full cost of the item (refer to current catalog for pricing)
- If the book is found later, a 50% refund will be issued
- Repeat offenders may face temporary suspension from borrowing privileges

### Damaged Books
- Minor damage: Repair cost up to $25
- Moderate damage: Repair cost up to $75
- Severe damage: Full replacement cost
Damage claims must be submitted within 14 days of discovery.

## Common Situations
### Example Scenario 1: Renewal Conflict
A student borrows a popular textbook that's needed for two different courses. The library will prioritize the course with the earlier due date, and the second user may need to request an interlibrary loan.

### Example Scenario 2: Holiday Borrowing
During winter break, all items must be returned by December 25th. Emergency loans for essential textbooks can be requested through the academic advising office with proper documentation.

### Common Mistakes
- Failing to renew items before they expire
- Returning books in damaged condition
- Not checking for holds on requested materials
- Using expired library cards

### Frequently Misunderstood Rules
1. **Reserve Items**: These cannot be renewed and must be returned by the due date to ensure availability for other users.
2. **E-book Limits**: While e-books can be renewed, they are not available during scheduled maintenance windows (typically 2-4 AM).
3. **Graduation Clearance**: All borrowed materials must be returned before graduation ceremonies to avoid permanent holds on academic records.
