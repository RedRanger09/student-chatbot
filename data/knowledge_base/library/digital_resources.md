# Digital Resources Guide

## Overview
The University's Digital Library offers a comprehensive collection of academic resources to support research, learning, and scholarly activity. This guide outlines how students can access and utilize online academic resources, including e-books, journals, databases, theses, and institutional repositories. All services are designed to enhance your educational experience while adhering to copyright and usage policies.

## Available Resources
### E-Books
Access thousands of full-text e-books across all disciplines through the **UniAccess Portal**. Search by title, author, or subject to find resources for course assignments or independent research.

### E-Journals
Explore peer-reviewed journals and periodicals via **Research Nexus**, a curated collection of academic publishers. Use advanced filters to narrow results by publication date, article type, and accessibility.

### Research Databases
Leverage specialized databases like:
- **ProQuest Central** (General academic research)
- **IEEE Xplore** (Engineering & technology)
- **JSTOR** (Humanities & social sciences)
- **PubMed Central** (Health sciences)

These databases provide access to scholarly articles, conference proceedings, and technical reports.

### Online Theses
Discover graduate theses and dissertations in the **University Repository**, a searchable archive of completed research projects. These resources offer insights into original scholarship across disciplines.

### Institutional Repository
The **Digital Archives Hub** hosts university-generated content including faculty publications, datasets, and multimedia resources. This repository is a valuable tool for finding locally produced academic materials.

### Digital Archives
Explore historical documents, rare manuscripts, and digitized collections in the **Special Collections Archive**. These resources are ideal for research projects requiring primary source material.

## Access Methods
### Student Login
Access all digital resources via the **UniAccess Portal** using your student credentials. Log in at [https://university.edu/login](https://university.edu/login) to authenticate and gain access to subscribed services.

### Remote Access
Use the **SecureVPN Service** to access campus resources from off-campus locations. This service provides encrypted connectivity to university networks, ensuring safe remote access to digital libraries.

### Off-Campus Access
For non-VPN users, utilize the **Remote Access Gateway** which allows limited access to core e-books and journals. Login with your student ID to bypass proxy restrictions.

### Institutional Repository
Access the **Digital Archives Hub** directly through the university website without requiring authentication. This public-facing repository contains curated academic content for general research.

## Usage Rules
### Download Policies
- E-books: Limited to 5 downloads per title per semester
- Journal articles: Single-use for personal study
- Theses/dissertations: Non-commercial use only
- Repository content: Adhere to specific licensing terms

### Printing
Print limits apply to all digital resources:
- 10 pages per day for e-book chapters
- 5 pages per day for journal articles
- No printing allowed for repository content without explicit permission

### Access Restrictions
- Remote access requires valid student credentials
- Institutional repository content may have usage limitations
- Special collections require prior approval from archives staff

### Acceptable Use Policy
- Prohibited activities: Downloading pirated materials, unauthorized sharing, commercial use
- Violations may result in loss of digital resource privileges
- All users must comply with copyright laws and fair use guidelines

### Citation Tools
Use the **CiteRight Assistant** plugin for your preferred citation manager (EndNote, Zotero, Mendeley) to automatically format references according to APA, MLA, and Chicago styles.

### Research Support
The **Library Help Desk** offers:
- Citation guidance
- Database tutorials
- Research consultation
- Copyright compliance advice

## Troubleshooting
### Common Access Problems
1. **Login Issues**: Verify your student credentials are correct. Reset password via the portal's account management section.
2. **Slow Connections**: Use the **Bandwidth Optimizer Tool** to reduce data usage when accessing large files.
3. **Authentication Errors**: Ensure you're using the latest version of your browser and clearing cache regularly.

### File Download Errors
- **403 Forbidden**: Check if your download quota has been reached
- **503 Service Unavailable**: Try again later or contact technical support
- **File Corruption**: Use the **Digital Integrity Checker** tool to verify file integrity

### Citation Formatting Issues
If citations aren't formatting correctly:
1. Update your citation manager plugin to the latest version
2. Verify your source database is properly configured
3. Contact library staff for assistance with specific citation styles

### Remote Access Problems
- **VPN Connection Failures**: Ensure your SecureVPN client is up-to-date and try reconnecting
- **Proxy Configuration Errors**: Clear browser cookies and restart your device
- **Firewall Restrictions**: Temporarily disable firewall software to test connectivity

## Student Responsibilities
- Adhere to copyright guidelines when using digital resources
- Report technical issues through the **Technical Support Portal**
- Respect download and printing limits to ensure fair access
- Use citation tools responsibly to avoid plagiarism
- Follow acceptable use policies for all online services
- Provide feedback on resource quality via the **Library Feedback Form**
