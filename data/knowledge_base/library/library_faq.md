# Library FAQ

## 1. How do I become a library member?  
To join the university library, visit the main entrance during open hours and present your student ID. Fill out the membership form with your details, pay the annual fee (₹500 for students), and receive your library card. New members can borrow up to 5 books at once. Alumni may apply for a guest pass by contacting the circulation desk.

## 2. What is a library card?  
A library card is your access key to borrow materials, use study rooms, and access digital resources. It includes your photo, student ID number, and expiration date. You can renew it online or at the service desk. Cards are valid for one academic year unless suspended for unpaid fines.

## 3. What are the library's working hours?  
The main library is open Monday-Friday 9 AM-8 PM, Saturday 10 AM-4 PM, and Sunday closed. Branch libraries have slightly different schedules. Always check the OPAC or notice board for special closures during exams or events.

## 4. How do I search for books in the library?  
Use the Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC) at [library.example.edu](http://library.example.edu). Enter keywords, author names, or ISBNs to find materials. Filter results by format (books, e-books), subject, or location. Use advanced search options for precise queries.

## 5. Can I borrow reference books?  
Reference books are for in-library use only. They cannot be checked out and are located in the Reference Section on the ground floor. These resources include encyclopedias, dictionaries, and specialized journals. Ask staff for assistance locating them.

## 6. How do I renew a borrowed book?  
Log into your account via OPAC or the self-service kiosk. Select "Renew" for eligible items (books not reserved). Renewals are automatic unless another user has placed a hold. You can renew up to three times per item. Returns must be done by the original due date.

## 7. What happens if I lose a library book?  
Report the loss immediately to the circulation desk. Pay the replacement fee (₹200 for standard books, ₹500 for rare volumes). Provide proof of purchase if applicable. Lost items will be marked as "missing" in the system until resolved.

## 8. How do I reserve a book that is already issued?  
Use the "Place Hold" feature on OPAC. Choose a pickup location and wait time. Reservations are available for up to three days. You'll receive an email notification when the item becomes available. Holds expire if not claimed within two weeks.

## 9. Are e-books available from home?  
Yes, access the digital collection via [library.example.edu/ebooks](http://library.example.edu/ebooks). Create a personal account using your student ID. Download or read online using compatible devices. E-books have reading limits (50 pages/day) and cannot be printed.

## 10. Can I use the library without my ID card?  
No, all visitors must present valid identification. Students need their library card; guests should bring government-issued IDs. Unauthorized access to study rooms or computers is prohibited. Lost cards can be replaced for a fee at the service desk.

## 11. What are the late fees for overdue books?  
A ₹5/day charge applies to each overdue item (maximum ₹20 per book). Fines accrue from the due date. You can pay online via the OPAC or at the circulation desk. Unpaid fines may result in account suspension after 30 days.

## 12. How do I return books late?  
Place items in the designated return slot by the due date. Late returns are still subject to fines. If unable to return on time, contact the library to arrange a penalty-free extension (available twice per semester). Regular returns help maintain collection availability.

## 13. Can I bring personal devices to the library?  
Yes, but only for educational purposes. Wi-Fi is available free of charge. Laptops, tablets, and e-readers are permitted in public areas. No audio/video playback allowed in study rooms. Charging stations are located near each branch.

## 14. How do I print from a computer?  
Use the self-service printers in the Technology Commons. Log into your account with your library card number. Select "Print" from any campus computer. Printing is ₹0.50 per page for black/white, ₹2/page for color. Free printing is available for thesis-related work.

## 15. Can I photocopy materials?  
Yes, but only for academic use. The photocopier in the Resource Center allows 10 pages/day with a ₹5 fee. Copying entire books or journal articles is prohibited without permission. Use the "Scan & Email" feature for digital copies of single pages.

## 16. What is the Wi-Fi password?  
Connect to "Library-Net" using your student ID as the password. No additional login required. The network supports both 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands. Coverage extends throughout all buildings. Contact IT support if you experience connectivity issues.

## 17. How do I get research help?  
Visit the Research Assistance Desk on the first floor or schedule a consultation with a librarian via OPAC. Librarians can help refine search strategies, locate sources, and evaluate information credibility. Group sessions are available for thesis writing and literature reviews.

## 18. What happens if my account is suspended?  
Suspension occurs after three unpaid fines. You'll receive a notice via email. Resolve outstanding charges within 7 days to reinstate access. Suspended users cannot borrow materials or use facilities until cleared. A $5 fee applies for reinstatement.

## 19. How do I clear my library dues before graduation?  
Submit a "Final Account Settlement" form at the circulation desk. Pay any remaining fines and return all borrowed items. Graduates receive a 30-day grace period to resolve debts. Unpaid balances may affect your degree certification until resolved.

## 20. Can I borrow books from other universities?  
Interlibrary loan requests are processed via the LMS portal. Submit a request for materials not available in our collection. Processing takes 5-7 business days. Borrowers must return items by the extended due date and pay any applicable fees.

## 21. How do I access online journals?  
Use the "Databases" section on OPAC to search subscribed journals like ScienceDirect and JSTOR. Log in with your library card for full-text access. Citation tools are available for managing references. Print or save articles directly from the platform.

## 22. Can I use study rooms without a reservation?  
No, all study rooms require prior booking through OPAC or the self-service kiosk. Priority is given to students with reservations. Walk-ins may be accommodated if spaces are available. Rooms have soundproofing and power outlets for laptops.

## 23. How do I request a book repair?  
Report damaged items to the circulation desk within two weeks of borrowing. Fill out a "Damage Report" form and provide details about the issue. Repairs cost ₹100-₹500 depending on severity. Severe damage may result in replacement charges.

## 24. What are the rules for using computers?  
Computers are for academic use only. No gaming or non-educational websites allowed. Users must log out before leaving. Each session lasts up to 3 hours with a 15-minute break between uses. Violations may result in temporary ban from computer access.

## 25. How do I request an extension on my due date?  
Submit an "Extension Request" form at the circulation desk with valid reasons (medical, family emergencies). Extensions are granted for up to 7 days. No extensions are allowed during exam periods or holidays. Late fees still apply if not returned by the new deadline.

## 26. Can I borrow books from the reserve collection?  
Reserve items are for short-term use only. They cannot be renewed and must be returned by the specified due date. These materials include textbooks and course-specific resources. Priority is given to enrolled students in relevant classes.

## 27. How do I find a book by its ISBN?  
Enter the full ISBN number on OPAC's advanced search page. Results will show matching titles, authors, and availability. Use this method for precise identification of rare or out-of-print books. ISBNs can be found on book covers or library records.

## 28. What is the policy on overdue fines for e-books?  
E-book fines are ₹10/day per item (maximum ₹50). These charges apply to digital materials borrowed from the online catalog. Returns must be done by the original due date. Unpaid balances may block future access to digital resources.

## 29. How do I get a replacement library card?  
Report your lost card at the service desk and pay ₹100 for a new one. Include proof of identity when applying. Replacement cards are mailed within 5 business days. Your account remains active during this period, but you may need to revalidate access.

## 30. Can I borrow books from multiple libraries?  
Yes, through the interlibrary loan system. Submit requests via OPAC for materials not available locally. Processing takes 7-10 business days. Borrowers must return items by the extended due date and pay any applicable fees.

## 31. How do I access online databases remotely?  
Use the "Remote Access" feature on OPAC to connect from off-campus. Log in with your library card number. Ensure you have a valid university network connection or use the secure VPN service provided by the IT department. Some databases require additional authentication steps.

## 32. What are the rules for using the library during exams?  
During exam periods, all study rooms and quiet zones are reserved for students. Noise levels must be kept to a minimum (whisper mode). No food or drinks allowed in reading areas. Extra hours may be added if needed, but check the notice board for updates.

## 33. How do I request a book recommendation?  
Visit the Reference Desk or use the "Ask a Librarian" chat feature on OPAC. Provide details about your research topic and academic level. Librarians can suggest relevant books, journals, and online resources. Recommendations are tailored to your needs.
