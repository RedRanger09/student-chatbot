# Library Membership Policy

## Overview
This policy outlines the guidelines, privileges, and responsibilities associated with library membership at [University Name]. It establishes criteria for eligibility, defines member categories, and details procedures for account management, suspension, and renewal. All members are expected to adhere to these policies to ensure equitable access to library resources.

---

## Eligibility
Library membership is available to individuals who meet the following criteria:

### Student Membership
Full-time undergraduate and graduate students enrolled in [University Name] programs are eligible for student membership. Proof of enrollment (e.g., student ID card or transcript) may be required during registration.

### Faculty Membership
Faculty members, including professors, lecturers, and research staff, are automatically granted library access upon appointment. Adjunct faculty must apply through the registrar's office.

### Research Scholars
Visiting researchers and postdoctoral fellows affiliated with [University Name] may qualify for temporary membership. A letter of affiliation from the host department is required.

### Alumni Access
Former students who graduated within the last 10 years may apply for alumni membership. Annual dues are required to maintain access.

### Temporary Memberships
Short-term visitors (e.g., conference attendees, exchange students) can obtain temporary passes valid for up to one year. A fee applies for this service.

---

## Membership Types

### Student Membership
- **Privileges**: Borrowing privileges (up to 5 items), access to study spaces, and digital resources.
- **Responsibilities**: Maintain accurate contact information, return materials on time, and comply with library rules.

### Faculty Membership
- **Privileges**: Unlimited borrowing, priority access to specialized collections, and interlibrary loan privileges.
- **Responsibilities**: Report lost or damaged items promptly and uphold academic integrity standards.

### Research Scholar Membership
- **Privileges**: Full access to research databases, lab equipment, and conference room bookings.
- **Responsibilities**: Adhere to data privacy regulations and report any security breaches immediately.

### Alumni Membership
- **Privileges**: Limited borrowing (up to 3 items), access to public events, and subscription to the university magazine.
- **Responsibilities**: Renew membership annually to retain privileges.

---

## Responsibilities
All members must:

1. **Maintain Accurate Information**  
   Update contact details promptly to ensure communication during emergencies or account changes.

2. **Respect Library Rules**  
   Follow noise policies, keep study areas clean, and use technology equipment responsibly.

3. **Protect Library Assets**  
   Report damaged materials immediately. Replace lost items at full cost unless a replacement is unavailable.

4. **Comply with Privacy Laws**  
   Do not share login credentials or access personal records of other users.

5. **Pay Fines Promptly**  
   Unpaid fees may result in account suspension after 30 days.

---

## Suspension & Account Blocking
Accounts may be suspended for violations such as:

- Repeated overdue returns (more than 3 instances within a semester)
- Unauthorized use of library resources
- Fraudulent activity (e.g., using another member's card)

**Suspension Process**:  
1. Receive a written notice detailing the violation  
2. Attend a hearing with library staff (optional)  
3. Reinstatement after 90 days of good standing  

Accounts are blocked for:

- Non-payment of fines exceeding $50  
- Reporting false information during registration  

Blocked accounts may be unblocked by paying outstanding debts and completing a mandatory ethics workshop.

## Frequently Encountered Situations

### Lost Library Card
1. Report the loss immediately to the circulation desk  
2. Pay a $10 replacement fee  
3. Wait 7 days before applying for a new card  
4. Provide proof of identity (e.g., student ID, passport)

**Note**: A lost card cannot be used for borrowing until the replacement is issued.

### Membership Cancellation
To terminate membership:  
- Submit a written request to the library office  
- Return all borrowed materials  
- Pay any outstanding fines  

Cancellation takes effect 30 days after submission. Alumni may reapply after 12 months.

### Misunderstood Policies
**Example 1**: "Can I borrow books from other universities?"  
→ Interlibrary loans require prior approval and may take up to two weeks.

**Example 2**: "What if my account is suspended?"  
→ You have 30 days to resolve issues. Contact the library director for appeals.
