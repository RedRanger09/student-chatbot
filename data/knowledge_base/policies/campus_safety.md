# Campus Safety Guide

## Overview
This guide provides essential safety information for students at [University Name]. It outlines emergency procedures, security measures, and resources available to ensure a safe campus environment. Students are encouraged to familiarize themselves with these protocols to protect their well-being and contribute to a secure academic community.

### Campus Security
The university employs a dedicated security team available 24/7. Security personnel are stationed at key locations, including entrances, parking areas, and public spaces. Students can request assistance by calling the Security Office or using the campus-wide emergency button network.

### Emergency Response
In case of an emergency:
1. **Alert Authorities**: Immediately notify campus security or local law enforcement.
2. **Follow Instructions**: Obey directions from safety officers and evacuate if instructed.
3. **Stay Informed**: Monitor official communication channels for updates.

## Safety Procedures
### Fire Safety
- **Evacuation Drills**: Conducted quarterly to ensure preparedness. Example scenario: A fire breaks out in a laboratory - immediately alert authorities, use fire extinguishers if safe, and evacuate using designated routes.
- **Smoke Alarms**: Located in all buildings; report malfunctions immediately. Common mistake: Ignoring alarm warnings due to noise or habit.
- **Fire Extinguishers**: Available in hallways and labs. Use only if safe to do so. Safety tip: Keep exits clear of obstructions.

### Medical Emergencies
- **First Aid Stations**: Located in each academic building and residence hall. Example scenario: A student collapses due to a heart attack - administer CPR if trained and call emergency services immediately.
- **Health Services**: Staffed by licensed professionals for non-emergency care. Common mistake: Delaying treatment for minor injuries that could worsen over time.
- **Emergency Transport**: Ambulances are on standby for critical incidents. Safety tip: Always carry a basic first aid kit in your dorm room.

### Evacuation Procedures
- **Evacuation Routes**: Clearly marked with directional signs. Familiarize yourself with exits. Example scenario: During a fire alarm, follow posted signs to the nearest exit and avoid using elevators.
- **Designated Meeting Points**: Students should gather at the main plaza or designated safe zones after evacuation. Common mistake: Not reporting to the correct meeting point, leading to delayed rescue efforts.
- **Special Needs Assistance**: Staff will assist individuals with mobility challenges during evacuations. Safety tip: Inform security personnel of any accessibility needs in advance.

## Personal Safety
### Night Safety
- **Lighting**: Use flashlights or phones for illumination when walking alone at night. Example scenario: A student is attacked after dark - immediately call emergency services and provide location details.
- **Avoid Isolated Areas**: Stick to well-lit paths and avoid shortcuts through empty lots. Common mistake: Taking unverified shortcuts that lead to unsafe areas.
- **Trust Your Instincts**: If a situation feels unsafe, leave immediately and seek help. Safety tip: Share your travel plans with friends or family.

### Women Safety Initiatives
- **Safe Walk Programs**: Free escorted walks available during late hours. Example scenario: A female student uses the Safe Walk Program to return to her dorm after curfew.
- **Anonymous Reporting**: Use the "Speak Up" portal for safety concerns without revealing identity. Common mistake: Delaying reporting due to fear of retaliation.
- **Emergency Contacts**: Pre-programmed numbers for quick access in emergencies. Safety tip: Keep emergency contacts visible on your phone and in your wallet.

### Cyber Safety
- **Phishing Awareness**: Do not click suspicious links or share personal information online.
- **Secure Passwords**: Use unique, complex passwords for all accounts. Enable two-factor authentication.
- **Reporting Fraud**: Report cyber incidents to the IT Help Desk at (123) 456-7892.

## Security Personnel
Security officers undergo regular training in conflict resolution and emergency response. They are equipped to handle incidents ranging from minor disputes to major crises. Students can request a security escort for late-night travel or report safety concerns through the university portal.

## CCTV Surveillance
CCTV cameras are strategically placed across campus to deter crime and assist in investigations. Footage is retained for 30 days and accessible to authorized personnel only. The Security Office can provide details about camera locations and viewing procedures.

## Lost and Found
Lost items are stored in the Main Administration Building, Room 205. Students can check lost-and-found notices on campus bulletin boards or use the university's online portal. Items are kept for 30 days before being donated or discarded.

## Disaster Preparedness
The university conducts annual drills for earthquakes, tornadoes, and floods. Students should:
- Review emergency plans in their dorms or classrooms.
- Keep a disaster kit with essentials like water, snacks, and a flashlight.
- Participate in community preparedness events.

## Safety Drills
Drills are scheduled monthly to practice evacuation routes, fire extinguisher use, and lockdown procedures. Participation is mandatory for all students. Drills typically last 15–30 minutes and are conducted during non-class hours.

## Student Responsibilities
Students must:
- **Familiarize themselves with safety protocols and emergency contacts**. Example: Review the Campus Safety Guide monthly and memorize key emergency numbers.
- **Report unsafe conditions or suspicious activities to authorities**. Common mistake: Ignoring visible hazards like broken fire alarms or blocked exits.
- **Participate in drills and follow instructions during emergencies**. Safety tip: Treat all drills as real emergencies to build muscle memory.
- **Respect campus security personnel and cooperate with investigations**. Example: Provide accurate information to security officers during incident reports.

By adhering to these guidelines, students can help maintain a secure environment for everyone. For additional resources, visit the [Campus Safety Portal](https://university.edu/safety).
