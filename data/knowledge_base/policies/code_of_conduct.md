# Student Code of Conduct

## Overview
This document outlines the behavioral expectations for all students at [University Name]. It establishes a framework of respect, responsibility, and academic integrity to ensure a safe and productive learning environment both on and off campus. Students are expected to uphold these standards in all interactions with faculty, staff, peers, and the broader community.

---

## Behaviour Expectations
### Purpose
The purpose of this code of conduct is to promote a respectful, inclusive, and ethical academic environment. It sets clear expectations for behavior that supports the educational mission of the university and protects the rights and dignity of all members of the university community.

### Student Responsibilities
Students are responsible for:
- Adhering to all university policies and procedures
- Respecting the rights and property of others
- Maintaining academic integrity in all coursework
- Participating constructively in campus activities
- Contributing to a positive learning environment

### Respectful Behaviour
Respect is foundational to all interactions. Students should:
- Use courteous language and avoid offensive speech
- Treat faculty, staff, and peers with courtesy
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or bullying in any form
- Give appropriate attention to others during conversations
- Maintain professional boundaries in all relationships

**Examples of Appropriate Conduct:**
- Engaging in respectful dialogue during class discussions
- Offering help to classmates without demanding reciprocity
- Using inclusive language that acknowledges diverse identities

**Examples of Inappropriate Conduct:**
- Making derogatory comments about race, gender, or sexual orientation
- Interrupting others during conversations or presentations
- Displaying disrespectful body language (e.g., rolling eyes, scoffing)

### Academic Integrity
Academic integrity is the cornerstone of university education. Students must:
- Cite all sources of information properly
- Avoid plagiarism in written work and oral presentations
- Submit original work unless explicitly permitted to collaborate
- Refrain from cheating on exams or assignments
- Report suspected violations of academic integrity

**Examples of Appropriate Conduct:**
- Using quotation marks for direct quotes in essays
- Creating proper references for all sources used in research papers
- Collaborating with peers on group projects under faculty supervision

**Examples of Inappropriate Conduct:**
- Copying text from online sources without attribution
- Submitting work completed by another student as your own
- Fabricating data or results for academic purposes

---

## Academic Conduct
### Classroom Behaviour
Students should:
- Arrive on time and be prepared for class
- Participate actively in discussions and activities
- Use electronic devices only for educational purposes
- Raise hands before speaking during lectures
- Respect the instructor's authority and expertise

**Frequently Misunderstood Expectations:**
- Asking questions is encouraged, but disruptive interruptions are not
- Collaborative learning is supported, but cheating is strictly prohibited
- Silence is expected during exams, but open dialogue is encouraged in class discussions

### Property Protection
Students must:
- Respect the property of others (including digital assets)
- Avoid damaging university facilities or equipment
- Return borrowed materials in good condition
- Refrain from unauthorized use of university resources

**Examples of Appropriate Conduct:**
- Using library resources responsibly and returning books on time
- Citing sources for any information used in academic work
- Reporting damaged campus property to the appropriate authorities

---

## Digital Conduct
### Online Behaviour
Students must:
- Maintain respectful communication in all digital interactions
- Avoid spreading misinformation or harmful content
- Protect personal and others' privacy online
- Refrain from cyberbullying or harassment
- Use technology responsibly in academic contexts

**Examples of Appropriate Conduct:**
- Participating in online discussions with constructive feedback
- Using social media to promote university events appropriately
- Sharing course materials only with authorized recipients

**Examples of Inappropriate Conduct:**
- Posting offensive content on personal social media accounts
- Sending unsolicited messages to faculty or staff
- Using university computers for unauthorized purposes

### Social Media Conduct
Students should:
- Avoid sharing confidential information about the university
- Refrain from posting discriminatory or harassing content
- Respect intellectual property rights online
- Maintain professional standards in all public communications

**Frequently Misunderstood Expectations:**
- Personal social media accounts are private, but university representatives must maintain professionalism
- Sharing course materials is allowed only with proper attribution
- Public criticism of faculty should be done through formal channels

---

## Responsibilities
### Respect for Faculty
Students must:
- Treat faculty members with courtesy and respect
- Attend classes and office hours when required
- Use appropriate communication channels for academic inquiries
- Avoid disruptive behavior during lectures or seminars

### Respect for Staff
Students are expected to:
- Acknowledge the value of support staff in maintaining university operations
- Follow procedures for requesting services or assistance
- Report any issues with campus facilities promptly
- Maintain a respectful attitude toward all staff members

### Respect for Fellow Students
Students should:
- Avoid actions that disrupt the learning environment
- Support a culture of mutual respect and collaboration
- Refrain from spreading rumors or misinformation about peers
- Encourage positive interactions in group settings

---

## Consequences of Misconduct
Violations of this code of conduct may result in disciplinary action, which could include:
- Warning letters
- Loss of privileges (e.g., access to campus facilities)
- Probation
- Suspension
- Expulsion

The severity of the consequence will depend on the nature of the violation and any prior disciplinary history. Students are encouraged to seek guidance from academic advisors or counselors when facing difficult decisions.

**Appeals Process:**
Students have the right to appeal disciplinary actions through the university's formal grievance procedure. Appeals must be submitted within 10 business days of receiving notice of the action.

---

## Final Notes
This code of conduct is not meant to restrict freedom of expression, but to ensure that all voices are heard respectfully and that the educational mission of the university is upheld. Students are encouraged to ask questions about expectations and seek clarification when needed. By adhering to these standards, we can create a learning environment that benefits everyone.
