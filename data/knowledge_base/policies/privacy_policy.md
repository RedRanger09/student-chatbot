# Privacy Policy

## Overview
[University Name] is committed to protecting the privacy of student information. This policy explains how we collect, store, use, and protect your personal data. By enrolling at [University Name], you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

## Information Collected
We collect various types of personal information, including:
- **Personal Identifiers**: Names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses
- **Academic Records**: Course enrollments, grades, transcripts
- **Financial Information**: Payment details, scholarships, loans
- **ERP Data**: Student ID, enrollment status, degree progress
- **Contact Information**: Emergency contact details
- **Cookies**: Technical data from our portal system

## Data Usage
We use your information to:
1. Administer your academic records and financial accounts
2. Provide educational services and support
3. Communicate with you about university matters
4. Comply with legal obligations
5. Improve our services through analysis of aggregated data

## Student Rights
You have the right to:
- Access your personal information
- Request corrections to inaccurate data
- Delete your data under certain circumstances
- Opt out of data sharing
- File a complaint with our Data Protection Officer

## Security
We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:
- Encryption of sensitive information
- Regular security audits
- Access controls for staff
- Data backup systems
- Incident response protocols

## Data Retention
We retain your information for the duration of your enrollment and for a reasonable period afterward, as required by law. Data is typically kept for:
- 7 years after graduation for academic records
- 10 years for financial records
- 5 years for ERP data

## Third-party Services
We may share your data with:
- Accrediting bodies for verification purposes
- Government agencies for reporting requirements
- Service providers for institutional support
- Law enforcement when required by law

## Cookies (Portal)
Our portal uses cookies to:
- Maintain session information
- Analyze site usage patterns
- Personalize your experience
- Prevent fraud and abuse

## Student Consent
You consent to the collection and use of your data for educational purposes. You may withdraw consent at any time, though this may affect your ability to access certain services.

## Common Misconceptions
1. **Myth**: All student data is public  
   **Fact**: Only authorized personnel can access sensitive information

2. **Myth**: Sharing data with third parties is always optional  
   **Fact**: Some sharing is required for legal and administrative purposes

3. **Myth**: You can request complete data deletion immediately  
   **Fact**: Deletion timelines depend on legal requirements and institutional needs

## Student Responsibilities
You are responsible for:
- Protecting your login credentials
- Not sharing your account information
- Reporting suspected data breaches
- Understanding the policy through official channels
