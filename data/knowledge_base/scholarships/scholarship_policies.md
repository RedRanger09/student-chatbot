# Overview  
Scholarships are merit-based financial aid programs designed to support students in achieving academic excellence while reducing financial barriers to education. These policies outline the guidelines, eligibility criteria, and procedural requirements for awarding, maintaining, and managing scholarships. All applicants and recipients must adhere to these regulations to ensure fair distribution of resources and maintain institutional integrity.

---

# Scholarship Award Policy  
Scholarships are awarded based on a combination of academic performance, financial need, extracurricular involvement, and other criteria outlined in the scholarship application. The award process is conducted annually, with priority given to students who demonstrate exceptional potential or demonstrated need.  

**Eligibility Criteria**:  
- Academic excellence (minimum GPA requirements vary by program)  
- Financial need (demonstrated through income documentation)  
- Community service and leadership involvement  
- Special talents in arts, sports, or technical fields  

**Application Process**:  
1. Submit a completed application form with supporting documents  
2. Attend an interview or submit supplemental materials as required  
3. Await notification of award status within 4–6 weeks  

---

# Renewal Policy  
Scholarship recipients must meet specific criteria to retain their awards for subsequent academic years. Renewal is contingent on:  

**Academic Performance**:  
- Maintaining a minimum GPA (typically 3.0/4.0)  
- Completing required coursework with no failing grades  

**Behavioral Standards**:  
- Adherence to institutional code of conduct  
- No disciplinary actions or academic dishonesty  

**Reporting Requirements**:  
- Submit updated financial information annually  
- Provide proof of enrollment in the next academic term  

---

# Attendance Policy  
Recipients must maintain full-time enrollment status as defined by the institution. Absences exceeding 10% of total classes without prior approval may result in scholarship suspension. Mandatory attendance at orientation and academic advising sessions is required.

---

# Academic Performance Policy  
Scholarship recipients are expected to uphold academic standards. Failure to meet course requirements (e.g., failing grades, incomplete coursework) may lead to:  

- Warning notice with a 30-day improvement period  
- Temporary scholarship suspension  
- Permanent revocation of the award  

Academic probation is documented in the student's record and may affect future eligibility.

---

# Multiple Scholarship Policy  
Students receiving multiple scholarships must disclose all funding sources. The total amount cannot exceed the institution's financial aid maximum limit. Overlapping awards may require adjustments to ensure compliance with federal regulations.

---

# Fee Adjustment Policy  
Scholarship recipients are eligible for fee waivers or reductions under specific circumstances:  

- Financial hardship (verified through income documentation)  
- Participation in approved research or internship programs  
- Enrollment in high-cost majors (e.g., engineering, medicine)  

Requests must be submitted to the financial aid office with supporting evidence.

---

# Refund Policy  
Scholarships are non-refundable under any circumstances. Recipients who withdraw from all courses without authorization forfeit their award. Partial refunds may be granted for:  

- Medical emergencies (with hospital documentation)  
- Death of an immediate family member (verified by official records)  

Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of withdrawal.

---

# Suspension Policy  
Scholarships may be suspended for violations of institutional policies, including but not limited to:  

- Academic dishonesty (plagiarism, cheating, fabrication)  
- Violent behavior or harassment complaints  
- Unauthorized absence from required classes  
- Failure to meet renewal criteria  

Suspension is typically temporary (6–12 months), with the option to reapply after fulfilling conditions.

---

# Cancellation Policy  
Scholarships may be canceled due to:  

- Changes in institutional funding availability  
- Non-compliance with program requirements  
- Fraudulent application materials  
- Recipient's withdrawal from all academic programs  

Notification is provided in writing, and recipients retain the right to appeal decisions.

---

# False Information Policy  
Providing false or misleading information on applications or during audits results in immediate revocation of the scholarship. This includes:  

- Fabricated transcripts or test scores  
- Misrepresentation of financial need  
- Submission of altered documents  

Penalties may include legal action for fraud.

---

# Misconduct Policy  
Recipients found guilty of misconduct (e.g., substance abuse, unethical behavior) are subject to disciplinary actions, including scholarship termination. Institutional review boards handle investigations and determine appropriate sanctions.

---

# Appeals Policy  
Disputes regarding scholarship decisions must be resolved through the formal appeals process:  

1. Submit a written appeal within 15 days of notification  
2. Provide evidence supporting your case (e.g., medical records, academic transcripts)  
3. Attend a hearing with the review committee  
4. Receive a final decision within 30 days  

Appeals must be submitted in English and include all relevant documentation.

---

# Special Circumstances  
Students facing extraordinary circumstances may request exceptions to standard policies. Approved scenarios include:  

## Medical Emergencies  
- Severe illness requiring prolonged hospitalization  
- Chronic conditions impacting academic performance  
- Rehabilitation or therapy requirements  

Documentation from licensed healthcare providers is required.

## Family Emergencies  
- Death of an immediate family member  
- Financial hardship due to family circumstances  
- Caregiving responsibilities for dependents  

Supporting evidence must demonstrate a direct impact on the student's ability to meet scholarship criteria.

## Leave of Absence  
Students taking an academic leave must submit a formal request and maintain contact with advisors. Scholarships may be paused during the absence, with options to resume upon return.

## Course Withdrawal  
Withdrawing from courses without authorization forfeits scholarship eligibility. Approved withdrawals (due to medical or personal reasons) require documentation and approval from the registrar's office.
