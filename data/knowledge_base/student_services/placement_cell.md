## Placement Process
The placement process at [University Name] is a structured journey designed to maximize student success. Here's an overview of the key stages:

### 1. Eligibility Criteria
To participate in campus placements or internships, students must:
- Maintain a minimum GPA of 2.5/4.0
- Complete all core curriculum requirements
- Submit a completed application form
- Attend mandatory orientation sessions
- Pass background checks and verification processes

**Note**: Final eligibility is determined by the Placement Cell based on academic performance and attendance records.

### 2. Registration
Students must register for placements through the university's career portal. The process includes:
- Creating an account with personal and academic details
- Uploading scanned copies of academic transcripts

- Attending mandatory workshops on interview techniques

**Registration Deadline**: April 15th for all undergraduate programs

### 3. Internship Opportunities
The university partners with over 200 organizations across various sectors, offering:
- Summer internships (June-August)
- Semester-long placements (January-May)
- Remote internship options
- Industry-specific training programs

**Application Process**:
1. Review internship listings on the career portal
2. Submit applications by the specified deadline
3. Attend interview rounds as scheduled
4. Receive offers via email within two weeks of interviews

### 4. Campus Recruitment
Annual recruitment drives feature:
- Over 500 participating organizations
- Technical and HR rounds for engineering students
- Group discussions and case study challenges
- Panel interviews with industry leaders

**Recruitment Timeline**:
- **March**: Company registrations and resume screening
- **April**: First round of technical interviews
- **May**: Final rounds and offer distribution
- **June**: Post-placement counseling sessions

## Recruitment Process
The recruitment process is a collaborative effort between the university and participating organizations. Key phases include:

### 1. Company Selection
Organizations must meet strict criteria to participate, including:
- Demonstrated industry reputation
- Competitive compensation packages
- Opportunities for career growth
- Positive employee reviews

**Company Eligibility Criteria**:
- Minimum of 5 years in operation
- Valid business registration documents
- Proof of recent recruitment successes
- Compliance with labor laws

### 2. Interview Rounds
Students undergo multiple stages to assess their suitability:
1. **Initial Screening**: Resume review and basic aptitude tests
2. **Technical Round**: Subject-specific questions and coding challenges
3. **HR Interview**: Behavioral questions about work ethic and career goals
4. **Final Round**: Panel discussion with company representatives

**Interview Tips**:
- Research the organization thoroughly
- Prepare 5-7 key questions to ask interviewers
- Dress professionally for in-person interviews
- Follow up with a thank-you email after interviews

### 3. Offer Acceptance
Students must accept offers by the specified deadline, typically within two weeks of receiving an offer letter. Key considerations:
- **Multiple Offers**: Students may accept more than one offer but must notify the Placement Cell immediately
- **Offer Letter Terms**: Review compensation, work location, and contract duration carefully
- **Renewal Options**: Some placements offer opportunities for renewal in subsequent semesters

**Important Deadlines**:
- **April 30th**: Final deadline for offer acceptance
- **May 15th**: Last day to request offer deferrals

## Student Responsibilities
Students play a crucial role in the placement process. Key responsibilities include:

### 1. Academic Performance
Maintain a minimum GPA of 2.5/4.0 throughout the academic year. Poor grades may result in restricted access to placement services.

### 2. Attendance
Attend all mandatory workshops, orientation sessions, and interview preparation programs. Absences without valid documentation may affect eligibility.

### 3. Application Accuracy
Ensure all submitted documents are complete and accurate. Fabricated information may lead to disqualification from placements.

### 4. Professional Conduct
Represent the university with integrity during all recruitment activities. Any unethical behavior may result in disciplinary action.

### 5. Follow-Up
Respond promptly to interview invitations and follow-up communications. Incomplete responses may delay placement opportunities.

## Common Mistakes
Many students make critical errors that can jeopardize their chances of securing placements:

### 1. Last-Minute Preparation
Students who wait until the final days to prepare often struggle with:
- Poor resume quality
- Lack of interview practice
- Insufficient knowledge of company requirements

**Solution**: Start preparing at least three months in advance.

### 2. Ignoring Company Guidelines
Not following specific instructions for each organization can lead to disqualification. Always review placement guidelines carefully.

### 3. Overlooking Soft Skills
Technical proficiency alone is not enough. Employers value:
- Communication skills
- Teamwork abilities
- Problem-solving approaches

**Tip**: Participate in extracurricular activities and leadership roles to develop these skills.

### 4. Failing to Network
Building professional relationships can open doors to hidden opportunities. Many placements are secured through referrals and networking events.

**Action Plan**: Attend industry meetups, join professional organizations, and connect with alumni mentors.

### 5. Not Seeking Feedback
Students who don't seek feedback after interviews miss valuable learning opportunities. Use each experience to improve your approach.

## Frequently Misunderstood Rules
Several placement rules are often misunderstood by students:

### 1. Offer Acceptance Deadlines
The final deadline for accepting offers is April 30th. Students who accept offers after this date may lose eligibility for future placements.

### 2. Multiple Offers
While students may accept multiple offers, they must notify the Placement Cell immediately to avoid conflicts with company policies.

### 3. Internship-to-Placement Conversion
Internships do not automatically convert to full-time positions. Students must apply separately for placement opportunities after completing their internships.

### 4. Resume Submission Guidelines
Resumes should be submitted in PDF format, limited to one page. Exceeding these limits may result in disqualification.

### 5. Attendance Requirements
Students who miss more than three mandatory workshops without valid documentation will lose access to placement services for the academic year.
