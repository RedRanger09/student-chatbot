# Student Services FAQ

## 1. How do I book a counseling appointment?  
Students can schedule appointments through the university’s online portal under the "Counseling Services" section. Alternatively, visit the Counseling Office during office hours (Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM) to request a slot. First-time visitors must complete an intake form detailing their concerns. Appointments are typically 30–60 minutes long and can be booked for individual or group sessions. Confidentiality is guaranteed, and records are stored securely.

## 2. Is counseling confidential?  
Yes, all counseling sessions are strictly confidential. Counselors adhere to ethical guidelines that protect student privacy, except in cases where there’s an immediate risk of harm (e.g., self-harm or abuse). Students can request exceptions for specific circumstances, which will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

## 3. Can I get help for exam stress?  
The university offers stress management workshops during exam periods. These sessions include mindfulness exercises, time-management tips, and peer support groups. Students can also access free counseling sessions or visit the Wellness Center for relaxation techniques like guided breathing and yoga.

## 4. How do I register for campus placements?  
Visit the Placement Cell’s website to create an account and upload your resume. Attend orientation sessions to learn about application deadlines and interview schedules. Participate in mock interviews and skill-building workshops hosted by the cell. Regularly check the portal for updates on recruitment drives.

## 5. Can first-year students use placement services?  
Yes, first-years can access placement resources starting from their second semester. They must register with the Placement Cell and attend orientation to understand eligibility criteria. Early registration is encouraged to maximize opportunities.

## 6. What should I do during a medical emergency?  
In case of an emergency, dial the campus ambulance number (123-456-7890) immediately. Inform security or staff at your location for assistance. The Health Center’s on-call team will provide immediate care, and arrangements will be made for hospital transport if needed.

## 7. Can I get a medical certificate from the university?  
Yes, students can request medical certificates from the Health Center for absences due to illness. Submit a doctor’s note or visit the center directly for verification. Certificates are issued within 24 hours and must be submitted to faculty/HR for approval.

## 8. How do I submit a complaint against a hostel facility?  
Use the online Complaint Portal under "Hostel Services" to file a detailed report. Include dates, specific issues (e.g., maintenance delays), and evidence if possible. The hostel management will respond within seven business days. For urgent matters, contact the Resident Welfare Committee directly.

## 9. Can I report harassment anonymously?  
Yes, use the anonymous reporting form on the university’s Safety Portal. Provide details about the incident, including dates, locations, and witnesses. All reports are treated confidentially, and the Office of Equity will investigate without disclosing your identity.

## 10. How long does grievance resolution usually take?  
Most grievances are resolved within two weeks via the Grievance Redressal Committee. Complex cases may take up to a month. Students receive updates via email and can request meetings with committee members for clarification.

## 11. Can I get help with resume formatting?  
The Career Guidance Office offers free resume review sessions. Bring your draft to workshops or schedule one-on-one consultations. Experts will assess clarity, structure, and keywords for industry relevance. Tips on tailoring resumes for specific job roles are also provided.

## 12. What are the steps for a mock interview?  
Register for scheduled mock interviews through the Placement Cell’s portal. Prepare answers using the provided interview guides. Attend sessions with faculty or alumni mentors who will assess your responses and offer feedback. Record your session for self-evaluation.

## 13. How do I prepare for campus recruitment drives?  
Research participating companies, review job descriptions, and update your resume. Practice common interview questions using the Career Guidance Office’s resources. Wear formal attire and arrive 15 minutes early to make a strong impression.

## 14. Can I get support for internship applications?  
Yes, the Career Services team provides guidance on internship searches. They host networking events and help students draft cover letters. Use the Placement Cell’s job board to find opportunities and apply directly through their portal.

## 15. What should I do if I feel overwhelmed by academic pressure?  
Visit the Counseling Office or attend stress-management workshops. Practice time-blocking techniques and prioritize tasks using the university’s productivity tools. The Wellness Center also offers free mindfulness sessions to help manage anxiety.

## 16. How do I access health insurance benefits?  
Submit a completed insurance form to the Health Services Desk. Verify coverage details with the university’s insurance provider for emergencies, outpatient care, and prescription medications. Benefits are valid for both on-campus and off-campus medical visits.

## 17. Can I get sick leave for mental health reasons?  
Yes, students can request sick leave due to mental health issues by providing a doctor’s note. Submit the form to HR with a detailed explanation. Leave duration is approved based on medical advice and academic requirements.

## 18. How do I appeal a disciplinary action?  
Submit an appeal letter to the Grievance Redressal Committee within 10 days of receiving the notice. Include evidence supporting your case and suggest alternative resolutions. The committee will review it and notify you of the decision within three weeks.

## 19. Is there a complaint portal for academic grievances?  
Yes, use the "Academic Grievances" section on the university’s official website. File complaints about grading disputes or coursework issues. Provide supporting documents and expect a response from the relevant department within two weeks.

## 20. How do I report ragging incidents?  
Report to the Anti-Ragging Cell via their anonymous portal or by contacting the Dean of Students. Include details about the incident, witnesses, and any evidence. The cell will initiate an investigation and take disciplinary action against perpetrators.

## 21. Can I get help with career planning after graduation?  
The Career Guidance Office offers post-graduation counseling sessions to help students transition into jobs or further education. They provide resources on job markets, skill development, and networking strategies for long-term success.

## 22. What is the process for medical certificate verification?  
Students must present their medical certificate at the Health Center for validation. The center will verify authenticity and issue an official stamp for submission to faculty or HR. Certificates from unaffiliated doctors are not accepted.

## 23. How do I access first aid services on campus?  
First aid stations are located in each hostel and major buildings. Visit the nearest station during emergencies for immediate care. For non-urgent issues, schedule an appointment with the Health Center’s outpatient department.

## 24. Can I get a medical certificate for a sports event participation?  
Yes, submit a fitness certificate from the university’s Sports Medicine Unit to the Health Services Desk. The unit will assess your eligibility and issue the document after a physical checkup. Certificates are valid for one year.

## 25. What should I do if I need an ambulance during an off-campus trip?  
Contact the campus ambulance service (123-456-7890) immediately, even if you’re away from campus. Provide your location and medical details. The team will coordinate with local hospitals to ensure prompt care.

## 26. How do I file a grievance about hostel facilities?  
Use the online Grievance Portal under "Hostel Services" to report issues like water supply disruptions or maintenance delays. Include photos or videos if possible. The hostel management will address your concerns within five business days.

## 27. Can I request accommodations for mental health challenges in exams?  
Yes, submit a formal request to the Academic Support Office with documentation from a licensed therapist. Approved requests may include extended deadlines, reduced workload, or exam adjustments. Review sessions are available to explain accommodations.

## 28. How do I access the complaint portal for financial aid issues?  
Visit the "Financial Aid Grievances" section on the university’s website. Submit details about discrepancies in scholarship disbursements or loan approvals. The Financial Aid Office will investigate and provide a resolution within three weeks.

## 29. What is the procedure for reporting harassment by faculty members?  
Use the anonymous reporting form on the Safety Portal. Specify the nature of the harassment, dates, and any witnesses. The Office of Equity will conduct an investigation and take disciplinary action against the accused without disclosing your identity.

## 30. How do I get support for academic stress during exams?  
Attend stress-management workshops organized by the Counseling Office. Use the university’s meditation app for daily practice. Visit the Wellness Center for personalized coaching on balancing study schedules with relaxation techniques.

## 31. Can I get help with creating a professional LinkedIn profile?  
The Career Guidance Office offers free LinkedIn profile reviews. Experts will suggest keywords, optimize your headline, and guide you on showcasing achievements. Tips on networking with alumni are also provided during sessions.

## 32. What are the guidelines for mock interviews in the Placement Cell?  
Mock interviews follow standard HR practices, including behavioral questions and scenario-based challenges. Participants must dress professionally and arrive 15 minutes early. Feedback is provided on communication skills, body language, and answer clarity.

## 33. How do I access campus recruitment drives for internships?  
Check the Placement Cell’s job board for internship opportunities. Apply directly through their portal with your resume and cover letter. Attend pre-placement sessions to learn about interview preparation and industry trends.

## 34. Can I get assistance in writing a strong cover letter?  
Yes, the Career Services team offers one-on-one consultations to refine cover letters. They focus on aligning content with job descriptions, highlighting relevant skills, and avoiding generic phrases. Sample templates are also available for reference.

## 35. What should I do if I face discrimination during campus recruitment?  
Report the incident to the Office of Equity via the anonymous reporting portal. Provide details about the discriminatory behavior and any witnesses. The office will investigate and take appropriate action while ensuring your privacy.

## 36. How do I apply for a medical certificate for chronic illness?  
Submit a doctor’s note confirming your condition to the Health Services Desk. The university will issue a long-term medical certificate valid for one year. Renew it annually by submitting updated documentation from your physician.

## 37. Can I get help with job search strategies after graduation?  
The Career Guidance Office provides post-graduation counseling sessions on job markets, networking, and resume optimization. They also host virtual career fairs and offer resources for exploring further education or entrepreneurship.

## 38. What is the process for verifying sick leave due to mental health?  
Submit a doctor’s note detailing your mental health condition to HR. The university will review it and approve sick leave based on medical advice. Regular check-ins with counselors may be required during extended absences.

## 39. How do I access first aid services during evening hours?  
Visit the 24/7 First Aid Station located near the main campus gate. Staffed by trained professionals, this station provides basic care for minor injuries or emergencies. For serious cases, they will arrange ambulance transport immediately.

## 40. Can I get a medical certificate for travel abroad?  
Yes, submit an application to the Health Services Desk with your travel dates and destination. The university’s Sports Medicine Unit will issue a fitness certificate after a health checkup, ensuring you’re fit for international travel.

## 41. What should I do if I need mental health support during exams?  
Attend stress-management workshops or schedule counseling sessions with the Counseling Office. Use mindfulness apps provided by the university to manage anxiety. The Wellness Center also offers peer support groups for students facing academic pressure.

## 42. How do I file a grievance about faculty grading policies?  
Use the "Academic Grievances" portal on the university’s website. Submit your concerns with evidence, such as graded assignments and feedback. The Academic Council will review the case and provide a resolution within four weeks.

## 43. Can I get help with creating a strong internship application?
